# Mission 12 · The Man Behind the Glass

Act II · The Nine-Minute War. Purpose (bible): reveal the Witness without resolving the mystery. Bible target 70
minutes (campaign plan 91); estimate about 85-95 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m12_man_behind_glass.js` (script), `src/world/env_m12.js` (map in three states, cast, music,
code-drawn signs, screens and the fracture shader), `tools/tests/m12.json` (playthrough, `?m=11`). Uses the shared
Echo Step (`src/systems/echostep.js`, owner M10).

## Story
Thursday 23.10.2049, 23:40. After Halden, Kai comes to Helix to make Rook explain bed 4-07. Rook agreed to five
minutes in Observation Gallery C. On the intercom Rook tells him to look down: behind the glass, in Test Hall C, a
grey-haired man in a long coat taps three notes on the glass, the tune Mira used to hum. "That man is not one of mine."
Lockdown. The man walks out and Kai chases him through the east run (the lockdown shutter jams at half a metre: Echo
Step through it), past the security node and a pursuit drone. At the end of the north corridor the man waits, then
walks into the wall: the old west service run, bricked up in 2031. The dampers end there; Kai switches to 2026, where
the run is still open (bare bulbs, wet formwork). The man changes state at the cable-vault trench; in 2049 that part
of the run is Records Storage B, a room sealed with no door, holding Rook's 2031 order to seal "Site Zero Artefact B".
At the west stair core in 2026, the night watchman Ben Asante says Kai is the second one tonight: the first signed
his book "K. Varen, returning" and walked into the hoarding. In 2196 that wall is gone: the ruined west atrium, its
ground floor drowned. Kai climbs the rubble, walks a girder, Echo Steps over the missing catwalk (Ife, a scout of
Sera's people, calls the man "the Grey Man"). In Core Ring B the trail goes cold. The field below throws fragments of
everything that passes back up the shaft, repeated and out of order: in Residue Vision, in all three states, Kai finds
the man on each landing of the sublevel stair with his band's elapsed time, reconstructs the route (2026, 2196, 2026,
2049) and descends, crossing each flight in the state the man crossed it in. Chamber II: a second Chronolith.
"Who are you?" / "The person you become when you refuse to let go." The man bears Kai's wrist scar under his band,
says Rook "found two" and buried one, says he was the voice in the station ("You didn't listen then, either"), opens a
fracture with the second Chronolith and is gone. Kai's band: "Signatures in field: two. Operator: K. Varen. Operator:
K. Varen."

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing: crane through Test Hall C to the gallery, letterbox, chapter card, Kai's setup. Intercom with Rook
   (or wait at the glass), the staged reveal of the man behind the glass, the three-note motif tapped on the glass,
   lockdown, he walks out.
2. **Chase through Helix in 2049**: glimpse down the east run; the shutter jams at 0.5 m and Kai learns the authored
   Echo Step crossing; the security node (crouch behind the crates: the guards' sight is blocked at crouched chest
   height; or leave an afterimage: both guards turn to it while it lingers); radio exchange (Hale or Brandt); the man
   waits at the end of the north corridor (staged) and walks into the wall; the pursuit drone (recessed doorways as
   cover). Detection fails back to the last checkpoint.
3. **Use a corridor that existed only in 2026**: the dampers end at the old wing, switching unlocks; the 2026 run, the
   trench crossed through 2049's sealed room, back to 2026 for the rest; Ben at the stair core.
4. **Traverse the ruined facility in 2196**: the atrium reveal (staged, the man on the far slab), rubble climb, girder,
   Echo Step gap; falling into the water fails to the last slab; checkpoints on each slab.
5. **Reconstruct the Witness's route from repeated fragments**: Residue Vision in Core Ring B across the three states;
   each fragment repeats its seconds on a loop in place; reading it stages the echo with the landing stencil and the
   band time; a repeated copy of landing 2 shows the field repeats. The reconstruction asks the state of each landing;
   a wrong answer gets Kai's reasoning and a retry, never a fail. The stair throws Kai back until the route is known.
6. **Enter a chamber containing a second Chronolith**: each flight's field only holds in the state he crossed it in
   (wrong state: an instability meter fills and the field throws Kai back to the last landing).
7. Ending: the quote, the choice (take his wrist / ask about Mira), the wrist close-ups (his scar, then Kai's), "Rook
   found two", the fracture, a short playable "Stop him" run, the fracture collapses, the two operator signatures,
   the radio, completion card.

Music: `m12-glass` (the Witness: Kai's motif slower and lower), `m12-chase`, `m12-2026`, `m12-ruin`, `m12-fragments`
(a note added per fragment read), `m12-descent` (steps down per landing), `m12-second` (both motifs, unresolved).

## Optional content

Three notes from the Witness in Kai's handwriting (east run 2049, the run 2026, slab A 2196). Rook's office (calendar:
the meeting booked with a man the system calls dead; the Site Zero photograph "A + B"). Records Storage B (the seal
order; the 2026 visitor book). Ben Asante: extra talk and **the visitor book**: sign it as K. Varen, a false name, or
not at all; the same book in 2049's sealed room then shows your 2026 entry under the Witness's older one (cross-state
consequence). Ife (scout of Sera's people): the Grey Man, and the clue "twenty-one, forty-four, then something with a
minute in it". The repeated fragment.
