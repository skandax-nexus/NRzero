# Mission 17 · The Witness's Archive

Act III · The Last Version of Earth. Purpose (bible): explain the original tragedy and the Witness's motive. Bible
target 70 minutes (the task brief asks for about 91 with the optional content); estimate 65-90 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m17_witness_archive.js` (script), `src/world/env_m17.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn plaques,
documents and the Stacks map), `tools/tests/m17.json` (playthrough, `?m=16`). Uses `src/systems/combat.js` (Combat,
owner M10) for the mimics and `src/systems/echostep.js` (EchoStep, owner M10).

## Story
After the Museum of Failed Worlds (M16) left an empty case for "this world", Kai follows the Witness to Site Zero, the
sealed level under Helix where the ring was found. In 2196 it is the Witness's archive: brass reels of Kai's lives on
shelving from floor to vault. In 2049 the same rooms are Rook's evidence store, sealed by order 31/07 in 2031. In 2026
the rooms exist only while an echo lens plays a recorded room.
- **Reading Room**: four booths play four lives as warm ghosts: REC 0001 *The First Morning* (the ring's own pickup,
  19.10.2026: Rook starts RUN 01 at 08:14:02, 4.1 kHz; Mira, ten, hums, slips under the rope and reaches holding Kai's
  hand; Rook aborts at 08:14:11), REC 0233 *The Boy Who Went to Halden* (Kai at 19, Ward 4, a pass stamped NO
  ADMISSION in red), REC 0640 *The Engineer Who Said No* (a Kai resigns in his mother's green ink; the Fracture came
  anyway), REC 1046 *The Last Try* (the Witness scratches a notch for every iteration: "Next time I won't change the
  morning. I'll change him.").
- **Rook's cages (2049)**: the RUN 01 and RUN 00 logs, the green-ink letter (our Kai wrote it too, and never sent it),
  Rook's black-ink letter closing the case.
- **The Stacks**: shelving walls on rails change every 16 s (layouts A, B, C); a pass and a band are in the far cells.
- **The Concordance**: lay on each recording's tray what proves it (decoys: the dry run, Rook's letter, Elena's 2026
  pass, an unworn band). Filing opens the Mirror Hall and hands Kai an echo reel.
- **The Mirror Hall**: mimics (ghost copies of Kai) fight in three waves; the archive's Warden reads which tactic Kai
  used most and counters it in the next wave; from wave 2 they Echo Step away from his bolts and speak his lines.
- **The Seal**: Echo Lens I plays Site Zero at 07:40, 19.10.2026 for 40 s; Kai switches to 2026 and walks the Control
  Room (run sheet: code 0108, breaker CV-3), the Cable Vault (breaker in 2026; Echo Lens II and its reel in the 2196
  pocket, 50 s) and the corridor door, into **Test Hall Zero**. If the echo ends in a room that only existed in 2026,
  he is thrown back to the lens.
- **Test Hall Zero / Room 0001**: the master lens (2196) plays REC 0001 where it was made and anchors the echo. Kai
  rebuilds RUN 01 in 2026: the console (4.1 kHz, start 08:14:02, abort 08:14:11), three collar dials (II · IV · I), the
  rope unhooked, and, in Residue Vision, where Mira, Kai, Rook and Elena stood. The reconstruction plays: Mira reaches
  at 08:14:09, the ring flares (soft, no flash), she falls, Rook aborts, the ring stays violet.
- **Ending**: the ring's memory: Mira's body ceased at 08:14:09.6; her neural pattern was captured at the same instant,
  61 % complete, persisting in the Chronolith. The Witness waits at his first desk: **"You kept bringing her back." /
  "I kept finding a world where she could exist." / "And what happened to those worlds?" / "They became the price."**
  He says every Kai builds the machine anyway, that this one came "to understand her, not to save her", and leaves.
  The band corrects the record: died 19.10.2026, 08:14:09; pattern incomplete; location the Chronolith.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing crane through the reel walls, letterbox, chapter card; the lift gate rises; Sera on the radio (if met);
   the band counts 1,046 recorded presences; the Witness's spoken welcome; 2049 unlocked.
2. **Match recordings to physical evidence**: four booths (staged ghost scenes with over-the-shoulder shots), four
   2049 objects in Rook's cages, four 2196 objects in the Stacks, the Concordance choice per recording (wrong answers
   explained and retried).
3. **Navigate shifting corridors**: the Stacks (layout timer HUD, slots glow amber 3.5 s before a wall moves, walls
   rising under Kai push him to his side, the Witness's map by the door and on the lectern).
4. **Fight security systems that mimic Kai's combat behaviour**: three waves; counters per tactic (takedowns → they
   watch their backs and cannot be taken from behind; stuns → shields; displacement → anchored; echo decoys → ignored);
   dodging Echo Steps from wave 2; an unarmed grabber in wave 3.
5. **Use temporal echoes to access earlier rooms**: Echo Lens I and II, the 2026-only Control Room and corridor, the
   breaker and the keypad code, the collapse back to the lens.
6. **Reconstruct the original Chronolith experiment**: anchor the echo, console parameters, collar dials, rope,
   Residue Vision placements, the staged run.
7. **Ending**: the ring's memory, the Witness, the quote, the corrected record, Sera, completion card.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Checkpoints: the Reading Room, the Stacks door, the Sorting Room, each mimic wave (restarts that wave with the same
counters, Kai healed), the Seal antechamber, the Cable Vault lens, Test Hall Zero. The Stacks cannot fail (walls push
Kai aside). Echo expiry in a 2026-only room returns Kai to the last lens (soft fade); the lenses replay any number of
times. Every question (four filings, the keypad, three console values) is retried with an explanation. Era switching
is refused with a toast where the other year is solid. Hints come as barks after 25-70 s without progress.
The echo timer pauses during dialogue and choices; the HUD (top right) shows the echo time left and the Stacks layout
with the seconds to the next shift. The mission shows the HUD itself after the intro, so it works as a first mission.
