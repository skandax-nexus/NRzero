# Mission 7 · The Broken Observatory

Act I · The World That Remembers. Purpose (bible): expand vertical exploration and celestial logic. Bible target 65
minutes; campaign plan ~85 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m07_broken_observatory.js` (script, scavengers, hall), `src/world/env_m7.js` (map, cast, music),
`src/systems/alignment.js` (the shared celestial alignment system), `tools/tests/m7.json` (playthrough).

## Story
Niko brings Kai up to the Sky House, the observatory on the spur above his settlement. Seventeen steps lead up to
it, one cut for each time the stranger came. Sefa, the old Sky Keeper, says the house "listens to the sky, and tells
the stone beneath what it hears". It was built around the Chronolith. Its three instruments must each be told their
star at their hour, and each hour lies in a different state of the place. Kai aligns the Sun Gate at dawn c. 1000
BCE. He crosses the east rooftops through three ages to reach the exterior ring, and aims the Pole Sight at Polaris
in Helix's 2049 night (the keepers had kept that seat in the sky empty for three thousand years). In the 2196 dusk
he stops Dace's scavenger crew, whose rewind gauntlets throw whatever they hit three seconds back, and aims the
Wandering Ring at the evening star from a turning orrery floor. The house answers: the summit pillar sinks into the
Hall of Dates. Its seventeen stones count sunrises from the first fire through eclipses, a comet, the supernova of
1054, the Zero Second and 2196. The seventeenth stone carries Kai's mark and his birth date, 3 November 2018: "How
could they know when I would be born?" / "They didn't know when. They knew who." Behind that stone in 2049, Kai finds
a recorder dated 2041 in Mira's voice: the Chronolith doesn't record him, it has been waiting for him.

## Map (north = -z)
The approach and the seventeen steps lead to the lower court, with the West Hall (the mural), the keeper houses and
the East Terrace (the Sun Gate). Three stepped east roofs (R1 7.6 m, R2 10.1 m, R3 12.6 m) climb to the ziggurat
terrace. Above it, the exterior ring (16.6 m) circles the drum, with the Pole Sight at the north. The star stair rises
from the ring to a landing at the summit (21.1 m): in 2196 this is an open drum with a broken rim, a turning orrery
ring and the central pillar with the Wandering Ring. The Hall of Dates lies under the drum. The Helix site compound
(cabin, tents, generator, floodlights) is west of the approach in 2049.

States: **c. 1000 BCE dawn** (in use: braziers, banners, Sefa, Niko, acolytes), **2049 night** (Helix Site 3:
floodlights, fences, survey strings, scaffold, a canopy over the summit, a starfield turning around Polaris),
**2196 dusk** (ruin: moss, scavenger camp, plank bridge, the open drum with its orrery, Venus over the sunset).

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Establishing**: crane shot up the spur to the drum, then over the drum toward the dawn. Letterbox, chapter card,
   Niko at the foot of the seventeen steps (flag-reactive: `nikoObservatoryClue`, `m5_promise`).
2. **The Sky Keeper**: staged meeting with Sefa at the Sun Gate. Sefa explains the three instruments, and
   era switching unlocks (with a state toast).
3. **Align three instruments using the sun and stars in different states** (alignment.js `Instrument`):
   - *Sun Gate* (past only; target: the sunrise at 062°, 6° up, matching the real sky dome). Ring and sight marks
     are chosen from the engraved scale, and "Look through the sight" stages the view along the instrument and
     says where the target sits ("higher and to the left"). Aligned: the sunbeam lights the sun stone, the keepers
     pin the gate, and its weight-beam is lowered as the bridge to the first roof in every later age.
   - *Pole Sight* (2049 only; target: Polaris, 000°, 38.2°, the latitude on the Helix survey board). Looking
     through the sight runs a time-lapse: the stars wheel around the pole, and the one that doesn't move is the
     answer. Aligned: the star door opens in 2049 and 2196 (the past door stays shut, so causality holds).
   - *Wandering Ring* (2196 only; target: Venus, 272°, 14°, "the wanderer follows the sun down").
   Triad: a staged flicker through the three skies, the house answers, and the pillar sinks.
4. **Traverse rooftops, rotating platforms and an exterior ring**:
   - Roofs: the beam (after the Sun Gate), the stone stair-bridge R1→R2 (stands in the past only), Helix's scaffold
     stair R2→R3 (2049 only), and the scavengers' planks R3→terrace (2196 only; a 5 m gap that can't be jumped).
   - Exterior ring: broken at 55–78° in 2049 and at 28–45° in 2196, so the player walks it by switching state.
   - Rotating platforms: the 2196 summit's orrery (`OrreryRing`: two 62° bronze arcs turning at 0.22 rad/s between
     the broken rim and the pillar). It carries a standing Kai, and a pulse throws it back 3 s (a ghost shows the
     jump). The rim is broken at 195–225°.
   - Switching onto nothing is refused ("NO GROUND THERE IN THAT STATE").
5. **Fight temporal scavengers whose attacks displace objects backward**: readable cycle: idle → aim (1.3 s, the
   gauntlet brightens, drone sting) → a slow green pulse orb → vent (amber, 3 s, vulnerable) → takedown [E]. A hit
   throws Kai three seconds back along his own path (soft screen shift, rewind sting, "DISPLACED −3 s"). A hit on the
   orrery throws the ring back. There is a tutorial scout on the ring (2196), then Dace's crew on the summit (two
   grunts + Dace on the pillar, which can only be reached by riding the ring). Their gauntlets anchor the summit:
   switching is refused until they are down. Parley choice: let them go / take Dace's gauntlet / banish them.
6. **Discover dates that span thousands of years, including Kai's birth date**: the Hall of Dates. Sixteen stones,
   c. 1130 BCE → a blank stone "waiting". The real sky events (763 BCE and 585 BCE eclipses, 240 BCE comet, SN 1054,
   Tycho 1572, Krakatoa 1883, the 1969 Moon landing) are mixed with the campaign's own dates (Hensel 14 June 2026, Helix
   2029, Reservoir 7 Feb 2031, "a sister returns" 2041, the Zero Second, 2196). Reading five opens the seventeenth
   stone: Kai's mark and **3 November 2018** (consistent with M6: seven in June 2026, twelve in March 2031). The hall
   flickers to the past, and Sefa and Niko are there: **"How could they know when I would be born?" / "They didn't
   know when. They knew who."** Niko: "You are the seventeenth."
7. **Ending**: Sefa: a woman "with your eyes" left something behind the stone in the far time. In 2049, Kai finds a
   recorder, "REC 14.08.2041 · M. VAREN", and plays Mira's recording: "It doesn't record you, Kai. It's been waiting
   for you… It was counting toward you before they lit the first fire." Kai: "Not recording me. Waiting for me."
   Silence, the slow pull-back, the completion card.

## Optional content
- **Five temporal shards**: West Hall (past), R2 by the scaffold (2049), the scavengers' salvage pile (2196), the
  roots in the Hall of Dates (2196), and the sky table's reward (past).
- **The sky table** (a three-state fetch): three bronze star plates are scattered across the ages. The sun plate is
  in a boy's hiding place on R1 (past). The pole plate is in a Helix finds tray, "FIND 3-117", on the court (2049).
  The wanderer plate is in the scavengers' salvage (2196). Set back on the West Hall table (past), they show one sky:
  the sun just set, Polaris on the pole, Venus low in the west. Early November, Kai's era: "the night the count
  ends". Flag `m7skyTable`.
- **The mural of the seventeen** (West Hall): it reads differently in each state. In the past Niko joins with extra
  lines (`nikoObservatoryClue`).
- **Sefa's three conversations** (Tala's fear of the house as a weapon, "leave room for one more", "when the count
  ends").
- **The empty seat**: look through the pinned Pole Sight in the past. It points at an empty pole (precession).
- **Residue at the Pole Sight** (2049, Residue Vision): a woman's echo adjusting the sight, explaining Helix's "reset
  every night". This foreshadows the recording.
- **Helix Site 3 log** (2049 cabin, voiced): Rook closed the site in 2031 and the Sun Gate pin is dated 1010 BCE ± 40,
  "the chamber beneath the drum is not to be opened".
- **Eleven more stones** in the Hall of Dates (each adds evidence).
- **Scavenger choice** (three outcomes).

## Shared system: src/systems/alignment.js
- `bearingDir(bearing, alt)` / `dirBearing(v)` / `rzAzimuth(bearing)` (realism sky azimuth) / `angleBetween` /
  `relativeWords`.
- `StarField({ stars, background, seed, pole, opacity })`: named stars at authored bearings plus a seeded
  background in one draw call. It turns around the pole (`spin`) and can ring a star (`mark`).
- `Instrument(game, { name, yaw, pitch, bearings, alts, target, tolerance, describe })`: a two-axis sighting
  instrument driven by engraved marks through `game.choice` (mouse, touch and scripted tests). It has `.operate()`,
  `.look()`, `.sight()`, `.aligned`, `.error()` and `.locked`.
- `OrreryRing(game, { center, y, r0, r1, arcs, speed, states, mesh })`: rotating arc platforms with per-frame walk
  boxes. It carries the player, `.onSegment(x, z)`, `.displace(sec)` (temporal hits) and `.dispose()`.
- `ask(game, id, text, options)`: `game.choice` that consumes `game.autoQueue` in `?auto` runs.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Checkpoints: the start, Sefa, after the Sun Gate (terrace), each roof on arrival (R1/R2/R3, with the state
recorded), the terrace after the plank, the ring stair top, the Pole Sight, the star door, the summit landing
(scavengers reset to idle), after the parley, and the hall. Falling off roofs or the ring only drops Kai to a lower
level, from which every route leads back up. Falling into the 2196 drum pit fails back to the summit checkpoint.
Scavenger hits never kill: they cost three seconds of position. Every crossing exists in at least one state, and
the switch guard prevents switching into empty air. Stall hints come from Kai or Sefa after 45–90 s for each step.

## Budgets
Static geometry is merged per material per state (`Batch`), and state groups are toggled. There is one shadow
caster (the sun or moon; off at gfx=low), a hemisphere light and three point lights that follow the nearest light
sources of the current state (a constant count, so there are no shader recompiles). Each starfield is one draw call.
The hall group renders only inside the hall. At gfx=low on the court: ~40–90 draw calls and ~100–250k triangles.
