# Mission 15 · The City That Forgot Tomorrow

Act III · The Last Version of Earth. Purpose (bible): explore memory loss as a societal consequence. Bible target 65
minutes (the task brief asks for about 85 with the optional content); estimate about 80-95 minutes with the optional
content below.

Files: `src/missions/m15_city_forgot_tomorrow.js` (script), `src/world/env_m15.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn
documents and cards), `tools/tests/m15.json` (playthrough, `?m=14`). Uses `src/systems/loops.js` (LocalLoop,
loopRoutine; owner M6/M9) for the three tears.

## Story
2196. Sera brings Kai to the edge of Harrow Reach, a survivor city in the north end of old Rain City, and will not come
in ("I go in, I come out not knowing why my hands are shaking"). Inside the veil it is always Thursday 14 May 2193:
three years ago a storm of loose time (what the Fracture left lying about) came over the Keld hills, and Dr. Anja Vale
locked the city inside the last day she could be sure of, with the Lantern, a stabilizer built from an old Helix core
under the dome of the Hollow Archive. Eleven hundred people live the day again every morning and do not remember; Anja
does. Residents greet Kai with the city's one question: "Morning! First time in Harrow?"

The lock is fraying: three places replay the same minute and a half (tears). Anja sends Kai to read them and bring
the records to the Separation Board. In any order:
- **Harrow North station** relives its evacuation (loop 08:12:00-08:13:30): the PA sends everyone through the gates to
  Platform Two for the 08:14 coast service; nobody boards. Kai finds the council notice ("all departed safely on the
  08:14", dated 15 May: fabricated), Pip Ashe's father's driver pass (he drove the train out of Platform One at 07:56;
  six hundred people waited on Two: consequence) and, in the signal box, Tobias Marsh's log (he sent the train early
  because the line ahead was unmaking: cause). Marsh: "Every morning I announce it, and every morning I think I've made
  a mistake. Then the day ends."
- **Larkfield School** relives its abandonment (loop 10:29:00-10:30:20): at the bell Ms Adaeze Okafor fetches the order
  from the office, leads her class out to the gate, counts fifteen, and the children walk off to the Archive. The
  register (24 names, nine "not arrived · coast train": consequence), the council order of 07:40 in the head's office
  (cause, behind a corridor tear that pulses every seven seconds) and the term poster ("reopens 15 May, all pupils
  accounted for": fabricated). Okafor: "I always count fifteen. Why do I always set out twenty-four?"
- **The Saltmarket** relives an undocumented disaster (loop 16:39:00-16:40:30): at 16:40:12 the iron of the hall aged a
  century in one second and the roof came down; every afternoon the traders run from a roof that is already gone. Three
  aged girders point at one spot; under it Kai digs up the Lantern's feed cable, "FIRST IGNITION 16:40:12" (cause: the
  Lantern did it); Residue Vision shows forty-one people under the roof (consequence: no record names them); the
  warden's ledger says "inspected 15 May, no casualties" (fabricated).

At the board Kai separates the nine records into causes, consequences and fabricated evidence (every fabrication is
dated the fifteenth of May, a day that never came). Anja admits the hall: "Sixteen-forty and twelve seconds. I felt the
hall go. I told myself it was the storm." / "A lock can only hold a day people can bear to live again. So I gave them
one they could bear." Kai: "How many has it killed, Anja? The real number." Anja: **"That depends on whether you count
the worlds that never finished becoming worlds."**

At the Lantern console Kai chooses:
- **Stabilize the city**: he tunes the three rings to what really happened (07:56, 07:40, the Lantern's own 16:40).
  The tears close; Harrow keeps its day and forgets, but the record is true now (Okafor counts twenty-four). Anja stays
  as the keeper and lends Kai the Lantern key for the finale. Sera: "You let them forget. Like the world forgot what you
  did." (Sera trust -1)
- **Free it from the loop**: he releases the rings in the order the day happened (07:40, 07:56, 16:40). The Lantern
  goes dark, the plaza clock rolls to 15 May, and the city remembers: Marsh tells Pip the truth, Okafor counts the nine,
  Tully lays down his pears where the forty-one died. Residents leave with Sera's people; Anja comes with Kai. Sera: "You
  let them remember. Even the parts with your name in them." (Sera trust +1)

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Each tear is a LocalLoop: being carried by the crowd only pushes Kai back through the gates; the echo train, the
corridor tear and the ageing wave rewind the tear to its entrance (Kai keeps every record and every learned timing,
shown on the loop HUD). Checkpoints at the gate, the Archive, and the first entry to each district (kill plane at -6).
The signal box can be left from inside at any time; the office door is open from :16 to the end of every repetition.
Wrong board placements, wrong record comparisons, wrong cylinder speeds, wrong ring answers and wrong ring order are
retried with an explanation, never failed. Era switching outside the Archive is refused with a toast; inside it is
refused where the other era has something solid. Hints come as barks after 50-90 s without progress in each place.
