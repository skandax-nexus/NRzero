# Mission 16 · The Museum of Failed Worlds

Act III · The Last Version of Earth. Purpose (bible): humanize erased timelines. Bible target 55 minutes (the task brief
asks for about 72 with the optional content); estimate about 60-80 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m16_museum_failed_worlds.js` (script), `src/world/env_m16.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn labels,
documents and the photograph), `tools/tests/m16.json` (playthrough, `?m=15`). Uses `src/systems/loops.js` (LocalLoop,
loopRoutine; owner M6/M9) for the three memories.

## Story
2196, Harrow Reach. Mara Quill takes Kai down the stair under the Hollow Archive into the undercroft Edie Lark calls the
Museum of Failed Worlds: when a world is erased, some of its things come out of the dark down here, and Edie keeps them
("strays"). In 2049 the same rooms are Store B-3, the deep reserve of the Rain City Civic Museum, where crates hold the
current world's copies of the same objects. The museum's Founder (the Witness, never named until the end) built three
listening rooms; each replays the same morning, Sunday 19.10.2026, 08:12-08:14, from a world that no longer exists:
- **World 0031, the Quiet Morning** (Helix open day): Kai stayed home with a fever; Mira touches the ring alone at
  08:14:09. Artifact: her scorched visitor badge.
- **World 0207, the Reaching Morning**: both children; their mother is at the desk; Mira asks Kai to hold her hand,
  reaches, and Kai holds on; his right cuff tears on the ring. Artifact: the child's blue coat, cuff torn away.
- **World 0388, the Rain Morning**: a storm cancels the open day; nobody goes near the ring; Mira collapses in the
  kitchen on Calder Row at 08:14:00 anyway and the clock stops. Artifact: the stopped kitchen clock.

In the Concordance Kai lays each artifact on a tray (2196) and opens its crate (2049): our desk log lists two badges
(Kai was there), our press photo shows the open day in sunshine, our coat (Elena Varen's 2041 bequest) is torn the
same way. At Accession Zero (a fragment of the ring, a thread of blue wool, Kai's badge "K. VAREN · ACCOMPANIED") Residue
Vision shows a ten-year-old's right hand over a seven-year-old's left; Kai determines the Reaching Morning (0207)
explains the evidence: ours is the original morning, every other world branched from it.

Edie opens the stacks. An "unmaking front" sweeps the aisle from the study door every ~15 s; Kai hides in side bays,
passes a fallen shelf in 2049 and a security grille in 2196, reads the register of worlds (1,046 worlds; 0412 has no
end, Mira lived to 58, shelf "STUDY") and opens the Founder's study with that number. In the desk drawer, face down:
the photograph of older Mira and older Kai on the Haldane sea wall, 2074, world 0412. On the back, in her hand: **"We
had a thousand chances. We only needed one that was ours."** The lectern register logs iterations 1030-1046 with one
change each ("tried the hospital", "rain") and "1047 · this one · Kai, 32 · ………"; the wall holds 1,046 Chronolith
bands with Kai's signature, and a recording in the Founder's voice ("Edie thinks this is a museum. It is a lab
notebook. ... If you are hearing this, Kai, you are one thousand and forty-seven. Leave room for the coat.").
**Ending**: Edie and Mara join him; the museum proves the Witness has repeated the experiment 1,046 times; an empty case
waits labelled "Accession 1047 · This world. K. Varen, 32. Pending." Kai: "Not this time. Not mine."

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing shot down the nave, letterbox, chapter card; Kai and Mara descend the stair; Edie's welcome (staged
   singles); Store B-3 (2049) unlocked.
2. **Play three short memories from different versions of one event**: each chair starts a LocalLoop memory
   (08:12:00-08:14:09; kitchen to 08:14:00). Kai walks the morning unseen and must notice three things (desk log,
   conversations available only in their time windows); if 08:14 comes first the morning slips back to 08:12 and he
   keeps what he noticed. The 08:14 scene is staged (soft halo, never a white flash).
3. **Compare artifacts with the current world**: tray (2196) + crate (2049) + a three-way question each; wrong answers
   get Kai's reasoning and a retry.
4. **Determine which version best explains the museum evidence**: Accession Zero, Residue Vision prints, the choice of
   world (0207); wrong answers explained, retried.
5. The stacks (timing + era traversal, two mid checkpoints), the register of worlds, the dial lock (0412).
6. **Find a photograph of older Kai and Mira**: the drawer, close-up of the photo (portraits rendered from the cast),
   the back with the quote, a beat of silence.
7. **Ending**: the lectern register, the wall of bands and the Founder's recording; Edie and Mara in the study; the
   empty case 1047; the band's correction "the Founder of this museum is the Witness. Iterations logged: 1,046";
   completion card.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Checkpoints at the Keeper's hall, the rotunda, the stacks entrance, bays D-04 and N3, and inside the study. Memories
never fail: missing details only rewinds the morning (LocalLoop reset, knowledge kept, HUD shows what Kai noticed).
The unmaking front sends Kai back to the last checkpoint (soft fade) and resets its timer. Every question (three
comparisons, the determination, the dial) is retried with an explanation; the dial can be left ("Step back").
Era switching is refused with a toast where the other year is solid. Hints come as barks after 45-70 s without progress.
