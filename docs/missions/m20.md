# Mission 20 · Mira's Memory

Act IV · The Unwritten Second. Purpose (bible): reveal the emotional truth behind Mira's identity. Bible target 55
minutes (the task brief asks for about 72 with the optional content); estimate 58-78 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m20_miras_memory.js` (script), `src/world/env_m20.js` (cast, five music themes, code-drawn
tablets, papers and signs, the four maps), `src/systems/memory.js` (shared system, owner M20), `tools/tests/m20.json`
(playthrough, `?m=19`). Each memory is its own scene, built when Kai walks through its door (the previous scene is
freed). Development only: `?m20part=N` starts at the hub without the intro: 2 (no memory done), 3 (Calder Row
done), 4 (Calder Row and Test Hall done), 5 (all three done, at the Loom).

## Shared system: src/systems/memory.js
- `Memory(game, { id, title, holders, initial, bounds, onEdge })`: a remembered place. Each *holder* (whose memory
  you stand in) is a temporal state, so keys 1/2 and Q switch between "as Kai remembers" and "as Mira remembers" and
  everything per-state (T.only, collision states, interactables with states, residue) works unchanged. `bounds`
  (per holder) are the edges of what that person remembers: Kai is eased back softly, never failed.
  `fragment()`, `contradict()`, `reliability()` record what was found (for M22's "reliable evidence vs subjective
  memory").
- `MemoryReplay(game, { from, to, seconds, events, onEnd })`: a remembered stretch of time on a loop with a clock
  (08:13:30 -> 08:14:09). Knowledge is kept across loops; nothing fails.
- `MemoryLoom({ rings, offsets, levers, contradictory })`: the alignment puzzle. Rings of anchors, coupled levers,
  spokes that align; a spoke may be marked contradictory, and the loom is solved **without** resolving it.
  `solve()` returns the shortest lever sequence (used for the stall hint).
- `memoryEdge(w, h)`: a soft haze wall where a memory stops.

## Beats (bible "Playable sequence")
0. **The Threshold** (hub, the edge of the Unwritten Second): establishing shot, chapter card, Mira picks up M19's
   ending ("you said you wanted to understand me instead"). Three doors in a lake of evening light, the Loom (three
   stone rings around a clock), three levers. Mira can be talked to between memories (4 optional conversations,
   unlocked one per memory). Two shards.
1. **Explore Mira's childhood with Kai: Calder Row, summer 2025.** Kai is six, Mira nine. Hide and seek in three
   rounds: round 1 behind the bins (both memories agree); round 2 she hides in the cupboard under the stairs, which
   exists only in her memory (switch to 2, follow her Residue footprints, F); "It's singing again" (the three notes, E
   B A). Round 3 she is on a sandbar under the bridge that only her lower river shows. Skipping stones (a timing
   meter, "count the splashes, not the sinking"): Kai remembers teaching her, she remembers teaching him. Home to No.
   14: the kitchen as Kai remembers it (Mum laughing, the radio) and as Mira does (a Halden General neurology letter,
   Ward 4, room 4-07: the bed of 2038), the drawing both ways ("ME · MIRA · THE MACHINE" / "ME · KAI · THE MACHINE").
   Teatime: "In that one, we both go home." Optional: pencil height marks that go on past 2026 in her memory (M 58),
   Mum's list in green ink, the red ball (Kai's memory only), K + M carving on the bridge post (echoes M18), 2 shards.
2. **Replay the original experiment: Test Hall Zero, 19.10.2026, 08:13:30 to 08:14:09.** A 96-second replay on a loop
   with a clock (HUD), rewinding at the ninth second and keeping what Kai learned. In Kai's memory a crowd of visitors,
   Mum and Rook; in Mira's a dark hall where the ring sings. Four details to find, two per memory: the visitor badge
   and Rook's console (RUN 01, 08:14:02, 4.1 kHz) in his; the ring singing the tune and the rope's hook unhooked a
   minute early in hers. Then "Stand where you stood": the experiment plays out from both sides ("Hold on to me" / "Let
   go, Kai. You're not coming."). Contradiction: he held on and it wasn't enough; she let go so he would not follow.
   Optional: the visitor list (she signed herself in, age 10), Rook ("four minutes from history"), her version of the
   console (an unexplained audio pickup: three notes), 2 shards.
3. **Experience a discarded timeline where Mira survives but Kai disappears: World 0907, Rain City, 14.11.2049.** Kai
   is the one who was taken in 2026; Mira, 33, a Helix researcher, walks home in the rain. Holder 1 is "as she lived
   it" (she cannot see him; the crowd walks through him), holder 2 is "the shape Kai left" (the gap: his bike at the
   stop, his chair at the noodle bar, his memorial bench, his badge that still opens Helix). Follow her (stay close or
   the memory frays and puts him back near her) through four stops, each answered by leaving something in the gap:
   ring a bike bell ("Twenty-three years and I still hear his bell in the rain"), sit in the chair she keeps ("Two
   bowls, please. One's for my brother"), leave the flat stone from Calder Row on his bench ("Splashes, not sinking"),
   badge in as K. Varen. Her lab: Operator M. Varen, Recovery protocol RUN 0907, target Varen, Kai (7). Kai steps into
   her ring before she runs it, she sees him older, and the world is discarded at 00:01 ("A world where you vanished
   has no Witness in it"). Optional: Jun Park (reacts to `metJun`), a lecture poster, the window of the flat he would
   have rented, three notebook pages, 2 shards.
4. **Align three memories without forcing them into one perfect sequence: the Loom.** Each memory gives four
   fragments (tune, hand, machine, ending) placed as tablets on its ring. Three coupled levers turn two rings each;
   the Loom is aligned when the tune, the hand and the machine meet on their spokes. The endings never agree (the
   spoke glows violet). Sealing asks: let them disagree, or choose one ending. Choosing one is refused by Mira ("If
   you cut me down to one story, you cut me") and scrambles the rings (recoverable: align again). Stall hint names
   the next lever.
5. **The reveal (ending).** Her cuff has never been lit. "All of them. 61 percent of the girl who died at Site Zero.
   The rest is stitched from every world he made where I lived a little longer." (lines from M16's photo and M17's
   reel if found). The bible quote: "I remember being your sister." / "That's not the same thing." / "Would it be
   enough if I said it was?" Choice (it's enough / I don't know yet / offer your hand). Mira feels the Witness getting ready to run the
   experiment again (M21 begins it); "Varen, Mira: reconstructed consciousness. Lives remembered: more than one." Fade, results card.

Cinematic: establishing shots and chapter cards for the hub and each memory, letterbox for every scene, staged camera
