# Mission 6 · The Clockmaker's House

Act I · The World That Remembers. Purpose (bible): teach local temporal loops. Target about 45 minutes.

Files: `src/missions/m06_clockmakers_house.js` (script), `src/world/env_m6.js` (map, cast, music),
`src/systems/loops.js` (the shared Local Loop system, extended by M9), `tools/tests/m6.json` (playthrough).

## Story
Every Helix site survey from 2031 carries the same margin note: "Ask Hensel." Kai finds Oskar Hensel's clock shop in
Rain City's old quarter. Hensel opens the door before Kai knocks: "You're late." He says Kai sat on his counter in 2026
and told him not to let him in. In the house, every clock races and skips the ninth minute. The house has three states
(2026 / 2049 / 2196), which Kai's wrist can read. A man with Kai's face, decades older, left something in the tower.
Through the looping workshop, a stair hidden behind a bookcase, and a tower clock whose three collars must each be set to
the hour that age keeps, Kai finds a reel recorded by that man in 2026: "When the child returns, do not let him see the
ninth minute." As the tower clock creeps toward the ninth minute, Hensel stops it: "Not today."

## Beats (all bible bullets are playable)
1. **Establishing**: crane shot down the rain-soaked lane to the house and its tower (dial at 8:14), chapter card, Kai's
   voiced intro. Optional: the neighbour's bark; the bracket clock close-up.
2. **The door**: Hensel opens before Kai knocks (staged close shots). Flag-reactive: Hale visited (trustedHale) or
   Brandt broke the door glass (door is boarded up, `trustedHale === false`).
3. **The shop**: the racing, minute-skipping clock wall; the bible quote "You ask what time it is. Wrong question." /
   "What's the right one?" / "Which version of the question are you asking?". Era switching unlocks for the house.
   Optional: 2026 shop with Lotte (8) at the counter (Debt 1), Hensel's 2026 ledger ("Varen, two children…"), a
   Residue Vision echo of child Kai and Mira at the counter, a temporal shard in the 2196 ruin.
4. **Observe a repeating mechanical arm and cross the workshop** (Local Loop, 20 s): reveal cinematic; the loop HUD
   (ring timer, iteration count, learned timings as ring markers and text). Knowledge is learned by watching: the arm
   sweeps the near side at 0:04 and the far side at 0:12, the drop-hammers fall in a front-to-back wave every 5 s
   (wait on the yellow gaps), the escapement gate opens at 0:09 for three seconds. Being caught rewinds the loop
   softly (no flash) to the latest restart point and keeps all knowledge; the music gains a layer per rewind
   ("loop memory"). Optional route: jam the drive gearbox with the wedge; at the next wrap the loop puts the wedge
   back ("the loop puts things back; only what you know stays"). The workshop is pinned to 2049 (no switching).
   Shard on a lathe inside the arm's reach. Reset-count-aware dialogue on arrival in the study.
5. **Reveal a hidden staircase by comparing room states**: 2026 has an archway with an iron gate; 2049 a bookcase with
   floor scratches in a quarter circle; 2196 a collapsed wall showing the broken stairwell. In 2026 young Hensel lives a
   20 s routine (second Local Loop, observational): he sets the mantel clock to IX, the gate unlatches, he looks up the
   stairs, shuts it and resets the clock. Talking to him closes the causal loop ("You said you would come back looking
   younger"). In 2049, setting the mantel clock to IX swings the bookcase open (staged shot).
6. **Set clock mechanisms to different positions in past, present and future**: the tower shaft has three collars, each
   reachable in one era only: lower from the 2049 floor, middle from Hensel's 2026 scaffold, upper from a fallen beam
   and rubble route in the roofless 2196 tower. Rule (plate + dialogue + service dial): "Each age keeps its own hour"
   → VIII (2049, stopped at 8:14), III (2026, running at three), IX (2196, rusted at nine). Tower music adds a layer per
   correct collar. Shard on the 2026 scaffold.
7. **Find a recording left by older Kai**: all three set → the house's loops stop (era flicker, the workshop falls
   silent, every racing clock stops), the shaft base opens on a reel. Voiced recording (older Kai voice profile):
   "…When the child returns, do not let him see the ninth minute." Hensel: "He had your face. Thirty years more of it."
8. **Ending**: the great dial creeps from 8:00 toward the ninth minute, tick by tick; Hensel reaches past Kai and stops
   it — "Not today." (or "Not today. Not before I see her." if Kai told him about Lotte). Silence, fade, the recording's
   last words over black.

### Optional: The Clockmaker's Debt
Three pieces across three eras: Lotte at the 2026 counter, her unopened letters on the 2049 desk (2031–2048), and her
plaque under the tree in the 2196 garden ("Lotte Hensel, 2018–2101, who waited for the clock to stop"). With two
pieces, ask Hensel about Lotte in the 2049 study: he stayed inside the loop for 23 years as its keeper ("Not money.
Years."). Choice: tell him she lives a long life (needs the plaque) / urge him to read the letters / say nothing.
Reward: the keeper's watch (the only clock that keeps the ninth minute).

## Local Loop system (src/systems/loops.js)
`new LocalLoop(game, { id, label, duration, restart, region, resetToast })`: `at(t, fn, { learn: [key, text], when })`,
`hazard(fn)`, `track(obj, props)` (objects restored at every wrap/reset), `learn/knows` (retained, persisted in
`game.flags.loopKnowledge`), `setRestart`, `onReset/onWrap`, `window/progress` timing helpers, `variant(fn)` for
per-repetition deviations, `frozen/paused`, a HUD ring with markers, and `loopState` (t, iteration, memory layer) for
music themes. `loopRoutine(loop, actor, keys)` drives an NPC through an authored routine that repeats with the loop.

## Music and voice
Themes (env_m6.js): `m6-house` (2049, felt piano + music box + clock ticks), `m6-house-past` (2026, warm major music
box), `m6-ruin` (2196, wind and a dead tick), `m6-loop` (workshop, 8 bars = one 20 s loop, one layer per rewind),
`m6-tower` (pendulum, bowed metal, a layer per collar), `m6-witness` (the recording: Kai's motif slower and lower),
plus the shared `memory` theme for the Debt talk. Silence at the recording and the ending. New voiced cast:
`oskar` (78, slow and low), `oskar26` (55), `lotte` (8), `recording` (older-Kai profile), `neighbor`.

## Playtime estimate
About 45 minutes for a first-time player: street and shop with dialogue ~8 min, optional shop states ~4 min,
workshop observation and crossing (several rewinds typical) ~10 min, study comparison and the 2026 routine ~8 min,
tower collars across three eras ~9 min, recording and ending ~4 min; the Clockmaker's Debt adds ~6–8 min.

## Budgets
Static geometry is merged per material per era; era groups are toggled; one shadow-casting spot and three point
lights are re-rigged per zone and era (constant light count, no shader recompiles); rain only renders on the street.
Characters dominate the remaining draw calls (Kai plus at most two NPCs visible at a time).
