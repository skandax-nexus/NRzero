# Mission 5 · The Village That Never Existed

Act I · The World That Remembers. Purpose (bible): show persistent cross-era consequences. Bible target 55 minutes
(campaign plan ~72 min with optional content). Main path ≈ 45–50 min for a first-time player; with the optional water
quest, the survey-line investigation, six shards and the side scenes the mission plays ≈ 70–80 min.

Files: `src/missions/m05_village_never_existed.js` (script, cast, music), `src/world/env_m5.js` (valley, houses,
wall, dam, machine, textures), `tools/tests/m5.json` (playthrough).

## Map
One valley near M4's settlement, authored in three temporal states that share terrain and layout:
- **Thriving** (c. 1000 BCE): plastered flat-roof houses on stone footings, lanes, plaza fire with storytellers,
  waterwheel and footbridge on the village channel, potters' kilns and barley fields on the west terrace, the
  terrace leat with three stone gates, the head works (sluice, windlass) on the northern shelf, the Water Ring above
  the Keepers' Door in the retaining wall. Golden-hour sky, drum/pluck/flute theme.
- **Abandoned** (c. 400 CE): ruined houses, regrown trees and long grass; without the diversion the river has
  eroded the lower (east) houses into a marsh. The terrace wall bulges; its weep holes are choked. Overcast sky,
  lone flute.
- **Flooded** (2049): Helix's Reservoir 7: concrete dam, outlet tower G-2 with valves and gauge, a survey buoy over
  the door, dead trees standing in the water, the caretaker's cottage and bean garden on the terrace, power poles,
  Helix notice. Piano/strings over the dam hum.
- **The chamber** under the terrace (behind the Keepers' Door): the buried machine (ceramic matrix, superconducting
  seams), carvings, offerings (thriving), roots (abandoned), Helix survey kit and tag (2049).

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Establishing**: crane shot over the valley, letterbox, chapter card, Niko and Kai on the west terrace ("There's
   no village on it. Just a reservoir." / "Then your maps are wrong.").
2. **Tala at the water steps**: the river is hungry; it once ran east before a rockfall; Kai's wrist pulls toward
   the Water Ring.
3. **Explore all three versions**: touching the Water Ring forces a guided tour through abandoned and flooded states
   (staged shots of the empty village, the dam, the survey buoy over the door, the landslide), Niko grabs Kai's arm
   and from now on follows him through every transition (breadcrumb follower, re-places himself after switches).
   Era switching unlocks (1/2/3, Q).
4. **The goal is found by reading 2049**: the Helix notice says the tower is "access by boat only" → Kai reasons a
   diverted river would need a levee that would survive as a causeway.
5. **Divert a river in the earliest state**: Tala waits at the head works; lever three boulders out of the eastern
   channel mouth (hold), then close the village sluice at the windlass (refused until the channel is clear).
   Staged shots of the river turning east. → Abandoned: lower houses intact + memorial stone of "the one who turned
   the river" and the bible quote: **"You keep changing the world." / "I'm trying to get home." / "Then stop changing
   it."** → Flooded: Tala's levee is now a causeway to the tower, the notice reads "TOWER ACCESS: ALONG THE OLD LEVEE".
6. **Reinforce a retaining wall in the middle state**: approach the bulging wall (staged), clear three choked weep
   holes (Residue Vision shows where water used to run), carry two fallen roof beams from the ruins to brace the
   bulge, drive the wedges. → Flooded: the wall stands under water; no landslide over the door.
7. **Release a pressure gate in the latest state**: walk the causeway to the outlet tower (checkpoint), the main gate
   refuses against pressure (gauge, placard); open bypass valves V1 and V2 to equalise, then release the gate. If
   the wall was not yet reinforced Kai refuses ("Drain it now and I only uncover a landslide"). Reservoir drains
   (staged shots), the drowned village surfaces, Niko: "My village. It is still here."
8. **Discover a buried machine far more advanced than its era**: open the Helix hatch (SITE 0, SEALED 02.2031),
   follow the tunnel, study the machine (empty core socket, clamps), survey tag "Core recovered, sent to Helix Lab 1.
   Authorised: E. Rook. Security escort: Brandt" (line varies with `trustedHale`). Compare in other states: the core
   still burns in its socket in c. 1000 BCE.
9. **Ending**: Niko: "Your Helix did not invent the machine. It found it." — the Telling's "people in white", Kai told
   the story sixteen times. Choice: promise to stop changing the world ("I'll try" / "Not until I find Mira").
   Kai recalls "Do not build the machine" if `m1warning`. Fade, completion card.

## Optional content
- **Quest chain B · The Water That Remembers** (Tala, Niko, Iru the potter): ask Tala about the fields; follow the
  water marks on the gate posts (blue hand = fields, red barred hand = houses); set the three leat gates so both
  fields drink and the kilns stay dry (wrong settings flood Iru's yard); read Tala's oral-history mural (bible sample
  dialogue "You ask where the river began…" / "A river cannot remember." / "Then you have not listened long
  enough."). Outcome: Niko's observatory clue (seventeen stars, `nikoObservatoryClue`), in 2049 the spring runs again
  and the caretaker's garden is green, and a straight gravel bar becomes a shortcut route across the reservoir; Iru's
  cup can be dug out of the abandoned kiln yard 1400 years later.
- **Survey line SZ, March 2029** (2049, Residue Vision): three Helix survey stakes replay the survey lead and Brandt
  ("It was a hillside. Write hillside." … "From scratch. Say it back to me."); the third only after draining.
- **Six temporal shards** (two per state, one only reachable after the drain).
- Residue echoes of the last families leaving (abandoned), caretaker Ingrid Holt's conversations ("Nobody ever lived
  here, they said." / Niko: "I lived here."), ambient barks, examining the door in each state, the survey buoy.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Start, after the tour (Water Ring platform), after the diversion (head works), at the wall (before and after the
bracing), on reaching the outlet tower, after the drain, at the hatch. Deep water and fast current are blocked with
a bark instead of drowning; bounds and steep banks push back to the last good position. Hints from Niko every ~85 s
of no progress, pointing at the next unresolved step. Order is free between the diversion and the wall; the gate
refuses until both are done, so no soft-lock. The objective line is always derived from the furthest open step and
refreshed on every era switch, so a cutscene in another era never hides an open task (lever/sluice, drains/beams/
wedges). Tala relocates from the water steps to the head works only out of sight (other era, far away, or both spots
off-camera), with a 3 s fallback so a player who arrives at the shelf directly is never left waiting. Niko catches
up a step behind Kai (away from the camera) after long separations and keeps ~0.9 m personal space.

## Test
`tools/tests/m5.json` plays the whole mission with `?m=4&dtcap=0.25&gfx=low&auto` (desktop 960x540 and `--mobile`
844x390): all optional content (water quest, mural, Iru's cup, six shards, three survey echoes, caretaker), the three
causal changes, the drain, the hatch, a real walk (held W) through the tunnel into the chamber, the comparison and the
ending. It ends with `m5complete` and 15 evidence entries.

  at the foot of the Sky House steps beyond the ridge. M6 explains his absence and M7 opens with him waiting there.
- Shards also add to the campaign total `shards`, like M6 to M8.
- With `trustedHale === false` the survey tag line calls Brandt "the voice I trusted at the service lift" (M2's lift
  intercom choice).
