# Mission 14 · Welcome to 2196

Act III · The Last Version of Earth. Purpose (bible): make the catastrophe tangible. Bible target 50 minutes (owner
brief 65); estimate about 60-75 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m14_welcome_to_2196.js` (script), `src/world/env_m14.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn plaques,
papers and signs, zone shader), `tools/tests/m14.json` (playthrough, `?m=13`). No shared system is owned; the mission
uses the engine's Residue Vision, checkpoints and the stability arc of the HUD (as the binding meter).

## Story
After the Sable Yards (M13) Kai's band throws him out of the 2196 Yards into the Meridian Reach, Rain City in 2196: a
beautiful, devastated morning of overgrown towers, a broken tram viaduct, birds, haze and trees on the roofs. The band
has no anchor ("Binding unstable. No anchor in range."), so it bleeds; if it fails he stays in 2196 for good. Sera, on
the radio she put on him, sends him north to Lantern Hall, the survivors' settlement, whose old Helix return beacon
has never worked and needs three parts. Joss Harrow (the beacon keeper) and his daughter Wren went out for them two
days ago and never came back; their chalk marks are on Kai's road.

Kai follows their trail up Canopy Avenue (a chalk arrow, Wren's dropped lamp, two sets of prints that only Residue
Vision shows, the chalk at the Meridian East stairs) and finds them on the platform, held for two days in a four-second
loop anchored by a 2049 ticket post lodged in the 2196 platform. He pulls it. Joss looks at his face: "You've got his
face... You'll see it soon enough. It's in the square." They go home ahead of him.

Kai crosses the broken span on a fallen gantry beam with an unstable zone breathing on its middle, frees the Line 4
tram's live power cell between its arcs, climbs Halcyon Tower's open podium (a zone on the second floor) to City Relay
4 and takes the one phase coil still "holding" (B-0814). In the Memorial Square he finds a bronze statue of himself
before the ring of a machine: "KAI VAREN · ARCHITECT OF THE FRACTURE", ringed by a wall of eleven thousand names. Sera
arrives: "It's been you for a hundred and forty-seven years." ... "We teach children your name before they learn the
names of the dead." Kai: "Then they're being taught wrong." Sera: "You'd be surprised how often I hear that."

The timing crystal: the square's stopped clock (08:14) has one; people leave stones at its foot. Kai takes it (the Hall
notices; Sera covers for him) or leaves it and fetches the other from the flooded Line 4 underpass under a strong zone
(Sera's trust +1). Lantern Hall knows his face ("That's the face from the square"); Joss vouches for him. At the beacon
the three parts go in and Kai sets the anchor second (08:14:00, the minute every clock in the Reach stopped at). The
beacon wakes, a beam stands over the Hall, and the band reads: "Anchor acquired. Helix Sub-Level 4, 2049. Binding
stable."

Ending: Sera: "You're the seventeenth Kai Varen I've watched stand under that statue." (Kai: Niko said the same,
three thousand years ago.) "Different years on their bands. Different scars. The same face." Every one read the plaque
and said it wasn't him. "Every version of you denies it. Every single one. It's the only thing about you that never
changes." "What happened to them?" "They went home. And the Fracture still happened."

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing: crane over the overgrown Reach, letterbox, chapter card; the tear opens and Kai falls out of it; the
   view down the avenue; Sera on the radio (the beacon, Joss and Wren, the waymarks).
2. **Traverse overgrown skyscrapers and ruined transit lines**: Canopy Avenue under the leaning tower face, the station
   stairs, the platform, the fallen beam over the broken span, the tram deck, the east exit, Halcyon Tower's podium
   (three flights, collapses), the Memorial Square, the underpass.
3. **Avoid unstable temporal zones**: seven zones that breathe (closed, a 0.6 s swell telegraphed by a brightening
   shell, open, a soft collapse), one that drifts across the avenue, one on the beam (closes completely), one on the
   tower's second floor, one in the underpass. Contact throws Kai out and strips 14 % of the binding. Residue Vision shows
   each zone's full reach and a sweeping marker of its rhythm (the ring turns green while it is closed). 2049 ghosts
   (a lamp post, a car, a commuter with an umbrella) stand inside them. The binding drains slowly in the open (9 min
   from full) and recovers at the survivors' green-lantern waymarks, which are also the checkpoints; at zero Kai drifts
   and comes to at the last waymark.
4. **Scavenge components to repair a return beacon**: the tram's power cell (hold E between its arcs; a hold caught by
   an arc shocks Kai), the phase coil (three coils, the panels show which still holds its phase; wrong ones crumble),
   the timing crystal (the memorial clock or the flooded underpass); fitting them and setting the anchor second
   (08:14:00; a wrong second is retried with Joss's and Kai's reasoning).
5. **Track survivors**: four trail marks (one only in Residue Vision, with the survivors' green echoes walking the route
   they took), the loop at Meridian East, the anchor found by its gold thread in Residue Vision, the rescue.
6. **Discover a memorial depicting Kai as the architect of the Fracture**: the staged reveal of the statue, the plaque,
   the wall of names, Sera's arrival and the bible quote; the Hall's reaction; the school slate.
7. **Ending: Sera reveals that every version of Kai denies responsibility** (at the lit beacon).

Music: `m14-reach` (the bible's 2196 cue: 70 BPM, wide strings, sparse piano, a slow pulse, no heavy drums),
`m14-zone` (glass and a slipping tick near zones), `m14-track` (following the survivors), `m14-memorial` (cello and

`m14-reveal` (piano and a low drone; silence around the key lines).

## Optional content
Five temporal shards (the drowned block's shore, the far end of the platform, Halcyon's first floor, the drowned
underpass, the cistern platform). The Rain City Herald of 19.10.2049 under glass, a Continuum notice, a scavenger's
diary (day 9,412), the tower directory, the plaque, the wall of names, the Witness's note behind a memorial stone
("They will tell you it was you. Don't argue with them. Argue with me. — K."), the Hall's school slate and ledger,
Ruth's chalk on the cistern. Talks: the trader (reflects M13's choice), the medic, the child at the slate.
**The Survivor's Garden** (the bible's optional mission): Ama Okonkwo's beds have been dry for nine days; open the
cistern line's three valves in the right order (beds, run, top: Ruth's chalk; a wrong order blows the joints and resets
them). If the ancient river was turned in M5, the old channel along the east wall runs too and Ama says the bible's
line: "The channel is running again. Nobody alive remembers who opened it." The underpass crystal is itself an
optional, harder route.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Nine waymarks (the landing, the avenue, the stairs foot, the platform, the east deck, the tower approach, the roof, the
square, the Hall's yard) set the checkpoint and refill the binding; key scenes set their own (the platform after the
loop, the east deck after the beam, the square, the Hall). Drift (binding at zero) fades to the last waymark with the
binding at 80 %. Zones throw Kai out (never kill). A fall from the beam drops to the rubble under the span (a bark sends
Kai back up the stairs); a fall through a podium collapse drops a floor. Wrong coils, wrong anchor seconds and wrong
valve orders are retried, never failed. Hints come as barks after 20-50 s without progress.
