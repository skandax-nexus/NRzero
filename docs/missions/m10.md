# Mission 10 · Enemies Who Remember

Act II · The Nine-Minute War. Purpose (bible): introduce faction conflict and Echo Step. Bible target 65 minutes;
campaign plan about 80-85 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m10_enemies_who_remember.js` (script), `src/world/env_m10.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn
screens), `src/systems/combat.js` (shared combat-lite), `src/systems/echostep.js` (shared Echo Step),
`tools/tests/m10.json` (playthrough, `?m=9`).

## Story
After the nine-minute city lets Kai go, the Continuum (2196) takes him. Block D4 is built into the ruin of Helix
Sub-Level 4, and Prefect Ima Adair has met him in sixteen versions of this day: "You have stood in this cell sixteen
times. You walked out of it in four. You never remember us. We remember you." Through the vent of the next cell Kai
meets Sera Voss, who runs the survivors the Continuum has not caught yet. When he gives his name: "In my time, your
face is on every memorial." / "For saving the world?" / "For ending it." Kai burns out the suppression collar on a
live 2049 conduit, walks out of his cell through 2049 (Helix's archive had no bars), and cuts power in the 2049
substation, which still feeds the Continuum's block 147 years later: the gallery services (the guards go to their
dead console in emergency light), the row circuit (Sera's bars drop) and the vault maglocks. Through the 2049 vault he
reaches the Continuum armoury, takes the survivors' captured displacer, and with Sera fights through the transit
spine into Testing Chamber 2, where the Prefect assesses him in two waves. Four survivors are held in unstable
loops there; Kai chooses to release them (the block hears it) or leave them. On the broken gantry under fire his
binding leaves an afterimage that takes the Continuum's shots: Echo Step. He steps across the gap and leaves the
block with Sera through the breach, over the ruined city at dusk. "We'll remember this one too, Varen."

## Map (north = -z), two authored states
- Gallery: 8 cells on the south (pearl partitions, gold force bars in 2196; archive racks and two lockdown shutters
  in 2049 that seal Kai into a bay), seven concrete pillars on the north (cover), the guard post (west), storage cases.
- Substation (2049, three breakers, transformer, Iven's note) / sealed relay vault (2196), north-west.
- Records vault (2049, blast door on breaker C) / Continuum armoury (2196: racks, the locker, the file, the gate
  console), north-east.
- Transit spine (barricades and a checkpoint arch only in 2196, an alcove bricked shut in 2196), the doctrine panel.
- Testing Chamber 2 (round, r 15): the emitter rig, pearl barriers (2196) and Helix test benches (2049) as cover,
  the Prefect's observation window, four glass loop cells on the south arc, the loop panel.
- Shaft: the shielded sentry post, the broken gantry (a 6.5 m gap over the drop), the breach in the outer wall, a
  ledge over the ruined city; the survivors' skiff.
- Exterior (opening and ending): the Helix ruin with the Continuum spire grafted onto it, ruined city blocks, dusk sky.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing: dusk over the ruined city and the spire, letterbox, chapter card, the PA intake line; the Prefect at
   the bars (staged close-ups, title line "We remember you").
2. Sera introduced properly: tapping from the vent, staged two-shot through the grate, the bible quote with silence.
3. **Escape a detention block using stealth**: collar on the conduit (hold E) restores switching; 2049 to leave the
   cell; back to 2196 behind a pillar; crouch and pillars against the visors (alert / search / return behaviour,
   "?" and "!" markers, detection bar). Being seen before Kai is armed puts him back at the last checkpoint.
   The loop-aware guard bark from the NPC bible plays near the patrol.
4. **Switch power systems across states to distract guards**: the 2049 breakers change the 2196 block. A: the guard
   post console shorts, both guards walk over and stay 55 s, the gallery goes to emergency red (their sight range
   drops). B: cells 3-4 open (Kai refuses to throw it while the lights are on). C: the 2049 vault door. The 2049
   dampers drain stability and pull Kai back when it runs out; switching into solid matter is refused.
5. **Use a captured temporal weapon**: armoury takedown (from behind, hold E) or slip past; the displacer (tap = stun
   into a gold time bubble, hold = displace three seconds back along the target's path); the two gallery guards come
   for the door: stun, then restrain (non-lethal), or they yield when alone.
6. **Fight through a testing chamber**: the transit spine (fight or flank through 2049), then two assessment waves
   (the second with a shielded Assessor: flank it or throw it with a held shot). 2049 hides Kai from them but the
   chamber drains stability fast. Checkpoint per wave.
7. **Choose whether to release prisoners trapped in unstable loops**: the loop panel, Sera's cost (the lift's
   stabilizer feed, the whole block alerted); release = a timing puzzle (cut each loop when the subject touches the
   glass, the ring turns green; too early just snaps the loop) and an alarm; leave = a quiet exit and Sera's "Nobody
   comes back."
8. **Ending / Echo Step**: the shielded sentry, the Prefect's squad on the gantry, the afterimage born under fire
   (staged), then playable Echo Step across the gap with the afterimage drawing the squad's fire; Sera's line across;
   the breach and the skiff; completion card.

Music: `m10-cell`, `m10-sera` (Sera's motif), `m10-stealth` (tightens with awareness), `m10-combat` (layers by
threat), `m10-loop` (a phrase that regains a note per release), `m10-escape`, `m10-echo` (motif with its echo).
