# Mission 22 · The First Second

Act IV · The Unwritten Second. Purpose (bible): combine all major skills in the final causal puzzle. Bible target 50
minutes (the task brief asks for about 65 with the optional content); estimate 55-75 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m22_first_second.js` (script), `src/world/env_m22.js` (map, cast, seven music themes, code-drawn
signs, papers, screens and clock faces), `tools/tests/m22.json` (playthrough, `?m=21`). Uses the shared systems
Inversion (`src/systems/inversion.js`, M11) and Local Loops (`src/systems/loops.js`, M6). Reuses read-only helpers from
env_m6 (Batch, Hensel's cast), env_m18 (signTex, paperTex, shard mesh), env_m19 (Orren, the 2026 visitors, the unbuilt
sky city, floating fragments, veil shader).

## Story
The Witness began the final experiment (M21) and Kai went into the ring with him. They stand in Test Hall Zero at
08:14:09 on 19.10.2026: the first second, which the Witness has stopped from starting 1,046 times. The hall's roof is
gone and the open-day tableau stands frozen (Mira, 10, reaching for the ring; Kai, 7, holding her other hand; their
mother; Rook at the console; visitors). Every place where the Witness tried to stop the Zero Second leans into the
hall through its broken walls. The chamber under the ring is sealed with a causal lock that wants four links.
- **I · The Valley** (west): he took the core three thousand years back and had the keepers wall it in ("Nobody digs
  in a valley that floods"). The lime seal (212 tally marks: attempt 0212) kept the chamber dry, and Helix's 2031 dam
  inspection pulled out an intact, still-warm core. *He kept it whole for them.*
- **II · The Old Quarter** (east): his first attempt was a letter to Hensel: "Do not let them in." At 07:52 Hensel
  sent the children away "up the hill to the Helix open day"; the ledger shows they were booked at the counter until
  08:30. *He sent them to the hall.*
- **III · Ward 4** (south): the three notes heard in bay 4-07 are not made there. Inverted, the line runs from a band
  with 1,046 notches to Test Hall Zero, 08:14:02: his call to bring her pattern home is the song the ring sang.
  *She followed the song.*
- **IV · The second itself** (the hall): sorting what Kai and the Witness remember from what the room recorded shows
  the rope was unhooked, not climbed; Kai's cuff did tear; a man in worn, future-soled boots stood behind the boy; the
  ring logged three unscheduled tones; Rook started on schedule and the override at 08:14:09 was "K. VAREN". The
  Witness (attempt 0730) opened the rope to reach the console and pulled the boy back: *she went alone*. "My first
  memory. I made my own first memory."
The four links open the stair. At the iris Kai sets the links in the order the second felt them (burial, warning,
call, hand) and in the order he made them (0001, 0212, 0730, 1046): every attempt answered the last one, and every
one is a cause. In the final chamber: "Causal chain: closed. First cause: none." — the bible quote, then Kai and the
Witness (together or Kai alone, the player's choice) let the second start: 08:14:10. "Zero Second: cause identified.
Operator: K. Varen. Attempts: one thousand and forty-six. Every attempt: a cause." The islands drift into the hall:
the merged landscape of M23 begins.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Establishing / story setup**: fade from black over the floating hall, letterbox, chapter card (ACT IV), the band
   reads 08:14:09 "Elapsed: none", a close shot of Mira's hand, the Witness ("I have stood on this floor one thousand
   and forty-six times"), a pan to the places outside the walls, the sealed chamber ("Lock type: causal. Links
   required: four"). Reacts to `m20answer` (Kai came "to ask").
2. **Infer a causal chain from the ancient village** (the valley, keys 1/2/3): chapter card; start in 2049 (the
   effect: survey board on the raft), go one state earlier (400 CE: down the landslide to the seal with 212 marks,
   Residue footprints of the keepers), then the earliest (c. 1000 BCE: the Witness's echo at the door, only in
   Residue Vision). Build the chain at the Water Ring: three sockets from four tokens (one contradicted by the
   survey: "the core rots"). Wrong chains get the reason and a hint after two misses. Switching rules: never into
   something solid, never onto nothing, never under the 2049 reservoir; walking off the pontoon puts Kai back on it.
3. **Read a repeating pattern learned in the clockmaker's house** (the Old Quarter): a 9-second Local Loop: every row
   of hammers strikes eight times and rests on the ninth count, each row counting from its own first strike (dial and
   lamp over each row). Caught: soft rewind to the last row passed, knowledge kept (HUD lines), music gains a layer.
   In the shop a 30-second loop replays 07:52 (the children's echoes at the door, Hensel's voiced lines, his ledger);
   the clock wall lights face by face and skips the ninth; the drawer opens only if its dial is stopped on the
   skipped face. Letter + ledger → deduction: where would they have been at 08:14? (wrong answers are reasoned out).
4. **Use inversion knowledge from the hospital** (Ward 4): chapter card, the reversed intercom, Inversion (X /
   INVERT). Returning hazards (gurney, crash cart, IV pole; touching one fails back to the ward door). The three
   notes use inverted acoustics (loud at the door, silent at the source; SOUND meter). At bed 4-07 the tone "is
   arriving here". The pulse visibly runs bed → window; in a reversed room the truth is the other way, so the four
   relays are inverted last-in-first-out (R4, R3, R2, R1, as the Halden lock). A wrong relay snaps the line back.
   Traced: the source is the Witness's band, the destination 08:14:02 in Test Hall Zero.
5. **Distinguish reliable evidence from subjective memory** (the hall, holders 1 Kai / 2 the Witness / 3 the
   record): five claims (the rope, the cuff, the hand, the song, Rook); four examine spots that show each holder's
   version; checking a spot in the record logs its claims; at the evidence board each claim is sorted "backed by the
   record" or "memory only" (two people remembering the same thing is still memory). Then: who pulled the boy back?
   The Witness: staged reveal across the three holders.
6. **Open the Chronolith's final chamber**: the stair opens under the ring (staged); the iris's collars in two passes
   (the second's order, then the order of his attempts); misses explain themselves. The iris opens on the chamber.
7. **Ending**: "Causal chain: closed. First cause: none." "There was never a first time." / "Only a first second."
   The bible quote: **"You were trying to stop me from becoming you." / "I was trying to find a version of me that
   didn't have to."** One breath of silence. Choice: touch the core together or alone. 08:14:10. "Every attempt: a
   cause." The islands drift in (M23's merged landscape). Fade, completion card.

Cinematic: establishing crane and chapter card, letterbox in every scene, chapter cards per island, staged shots
(Mira's hand, the seams, the stair, the reservoir, the seal, the echo, the lane, the tower dial, the drawer, the bay,
the line, the window, the three-holder reveal, the stair opening, the iris, the core, the merge), seven themes that
follow the beats (`m22-second` 48 BPM glass and drone with the E-B-A motif completing as links are found,
`m22-village`, `m22-clock` with a nine-beat tick whose ninth is silent, `m22-ward`, `m22-evidence`, `m22-chamber`
building with the iris, `m22-reveal` piano and cello), silence before the choice.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Hall start; each island's entry (after its intro); the valley after the chain; each hammer row passed (the loop
rewinds to the last row passed, never further); the ward door (hazard hits) and bay 4-07 after the bay scene; the
evidence start; the top of the stair. Falls off any island return Kai to his last safe spot (core rule). No beat can
soft-lock: every deduction accepts retries, a wrong chain / relay / collar resets only itself, holders and states can
always be switched back (stability regenerates), and the valley's basin has a way up in both early states (old steps
in 1000 BCE, the landslide in 400 CE).
