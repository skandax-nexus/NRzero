# Mission 19 · The Place Outside Time

Act IV · The Unwritten Second. Purpose (bible): test player mastery without ordinary wayfinding. Bible target 60
minutes (the task brief asks for about 78 with the optional content); estimate 60-80 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m19_place_outside_time.js` (script), `src/world/env_m19.js` (cast, five music themes, the
code-drawn symbols and their decoys, the maps), `tools/tests/m19.json` (playthrough, `?m=18`). Three scenes, each
built when Kai gets there (the previous one is freed): Meridian Avenue + Clock Square, the House of Rooms, the mirror
gorge + Concourse of Doors + Test Hall Zero. Development only: `?m19part=1..4` starts at a chapter.

There are no objective markers anywhere in this mission. Objectives are written as what Kai notices ("The road
returns. Find what repeats."). The HUD era readout shows the second instead of a year (08:14:07, :08, :09, "—").

## Story
Kai goes through the Chronolith into the second nobody wrote, between 08:14:09 and 08:14:10 on 19.10.2026. Here the
unbuilt Rain City (the Varen Library, the second spire, the river park) hangs upside down in the sky.
- **I · The road that returns (navigate by recurring symbols).** Meridian Avenue in a stalled golden morning, the rain
  stopped in mid-air. Orren sits at the tram stop ("Nothing here points. Things repeat. Learn what repeats... It isn't a
  machine. It's a habit." "Sixteen of you have sat on this bench."). Walking off either end of the avenue puts Kai on
  the other end. Four alleys; each lap one alley mouth carries Oskar Hensel's true clockmaker's mark (8:14, MCMLXI,
  HENSEL) and the others carry decoys (reversed 3:46, 9:00, 8:41) or nothing. The true turn advances the second
  (08:14:07 → :08 → :09); a wrong turn returns Kai to the tram stop. On lap three every visible mark is a decoy: the
  true mark is chalk, drawn by an earlier Kai, visible only in Residue Vision. The third right turn opens Clock Square
  (approved 2041, never built), whose clock is stuck between the ninth and the tenth second.
- **II · The House of Rooms (recognise the clockmaker's mark, the observatory pattern, the Helix badge).** Rooms are
  joined by what Kai recognises, not by walls: Hensel's workshop (three clock-marked doors), the observatory chamber
  (seven stars, pole star centred, notch east; decoys six stars, notch west, pole off centre), Helix Test Lab 4 (four
  white orbits; decoys Continuum gold hexagon, three orbits). Decoy doors loop back to the workshop. The Gallery of
  Three: Orren ("It isn't a rule. The house just remembers what you've been."), and a great door whose three dials must
  be set to the true marks (a menu of described variants; misses hold the door, a second miss points to the carved
  references on the west wall).
- **III · The Mirror Gorge (a bridge visible only in reflections).** The deck exists only in the lake. Kai walks where
  the water says; ripples show under his feet when he is on it; two decks drift sideways. Falls return him to the last
  safe spot (barks after the first and fourth). Kai has no reflection ("You were never written here. Neither was I.").
  Mira walks beside him in the reflection ("The rest of me is past the doors. Come and ask."). Halfway: Platform Two
  of Harrow North as an island, with Orren on the bench (checkpoint).
- **IV · The Concourse of Doors (a door that appears in several locations).** The door to Test Hall Zero stands alone
  in a plaza and jumps to another of five spots whenever Kai approaches. Orren: "Don't follow the footsteps. Follow what
  they refuse to step on." In Residue Vision the paving is covered with the footprints of every earlier Kai; one
  winding lane is untrodden, with the three symbols inlaid along it. Walking that lane to its end makes the door stay.
- **Finale · Test Hall Zero, 08:14:09 and a half.** The open day frozen: visitors, Mira (10) under the unhooked rope,
  Kai (7) holding her hand. Adult Mira waits. "Kai. You've been trying to reach the version of me that would forgive
  you." Choice (ask / "I only wanted you to live" / sorry), each answered. Ending: "Saving me and understanding me
  aren't the same thing. You've got very good at the first one... Come and remember with me. Mine, not yours. Some of
  them won't agree with each other." (leads into M20, Mira's Memory).

## Optional content
- Orren conversations: the sky city, "what are you" (square), the platform bench (fears the final choice will erase
  him; mentions the Witness's notches if `m18witnessEcho`), "what happens to you" (concourse).
- The Unwritten Record: four loose pages (avenue kiosk, kitchen, Platform Two, concourse tower); all four add a summary
  entry and a line from Orren.
- Six temporal shards (alley D, fountain, kitchen, ticket hall, Platform Two, under the observatory dome).
- The Varen kitchen, 2026 (behind the mirrored star door): residue memory of Mira drawing "ME · MIRA · THE MACHINE".
- The Child in the Station (behind the three-orbit badge door): the ticket hall; hum Mira's three falling notes
  (E-B-A) to send her home (up to three tries; she waits otherwise).
- Evidence: timetable, the Courier (three dates), Clock Square plaque, Kai's card "VALID: —", the three symbols,
  "look back across the gorge".
