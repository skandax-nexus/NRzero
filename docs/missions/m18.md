# Mission 18 · The Fracture

Act III · The Last Version of Earth. Purpose (bible): combine traversal, survival and cross-era consequences. Bible
target 60 minutes (the task brief asks for about 78 with the optional content); estimate 62-85 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m18_the_fracture.js` (script), `src/world/env_m18.js` (cast, music, code-drawn signs and papers,
the five new maps and the Lantern Hall dressing), `tools/tests/m18.json` (playthrough, `?m=17`). Reuses
`env_m14.buildReach` (Lantern Hall, the M14 settlement), `src/systems/combat.js` and `src/systems/echostep.js` (owner
M10). Each chapter is its own scene, built when Kai gets there (the previous chapter's geometry is freed), so every
place stays inside the phone budget (measured on `gfx=low`: 29-175 draw calls, 27k-333k triangles).
Development only: `?m18part=1..6` starts at a chapter.

## Story
The storms that started three days ago are the Fracture spreading: every place that held a Helix anchor is losing it at
once, and the land comes loose between 2026, 2049 and 2196.
- **I · Lantern Hall (2196).** The beacon Kai repaired in M14 is the anchor holding a storm front off the valley, and
  it is failing. Sera holds the gate and the carts; Kai brings out the thirteen still inside: Ama's gardeners (she will
  not go without the seed tin on the cistern platform), Hedda Lind's school (Lio, seven, is hiding: his footprints show
  in Residue Vision), Nadia Oyelaran and Tomas Brennan (an escort: they only move while Kai stays close) and Joss, who
  will not leave his beacon (choice: shunt the field cabinets with him for time, talk him out, or let him stay; Sera
  drags him out at the end and her trust drops). Optional: Ruth Abara and her husband at the wall of names (Wren fetches
  them if Kai does not). Joss's last request: take the anchor's heart. The anchor dies, the front comes through the
  hall, Kai runs for the gate, and because his band was anchored to that beacon it is torn loose: "Test Hall C, Kai!
  Bring the heart!"
- **II · Varrow Gorge (2026 / 2049).** The band drops him on the Eastway where the storm has torn the fill away. The
  gorge under the road existed only before 2031 (Helix sponsored its filling as a "logistics corridor"). Kai crosses
  the 2026 site hoarding where it does not exist (2049), starts the site generator, takes the haul ramp down the west
  wall (storm cells throw him three seconds back), has crane 2 lay girder G-7 across the river, rides the pier hoist and
  climbs to the half-built deck, then steps off its end in 2049, where the fill is. Optional: the carving "K + M · 2025"
  (a residue memory: nine-year-old Mira counting stone skips, "You count the splashes, not the sinking"), the site
  board, the office whiteboard, two shards.
- **III · The Eastway (2049, night, rain).** The storm has brought 2196's sea into the present. Surges every 34 s
  (siren, HUD timer): Kai climbs onto car roofs, the median, the lorry. A jack-knifed lorry closes the road (climb the
  cab and the trailer, or walk the 2026 haul strip where there was no lorry). A fallen lighting column has made a band
  of water live until isolator E-7 is thrown at low water. Jun Park's night bus N4 is stalled with six passengers on its
  roof at the dip under the Line 6 bridge (two metres of water). Pump station 3 trips: culvert C-3 has been blocked
  "since commissioning". In 2026 Kai pulls the timber formwork nobody struck before the pour; in 2049 the culvert is
  clear, the pumps run, the water falls away, and Jun walks his passengers out ("That's twice, Varen" if he met Kai in
  M1). Hale or Brandt (trust choice) on the radio.
- **IV · The Corran Building (2196; 2049 holds 12 s at a time below the third floor).** The tower lost its upper
  thirty floors last night; its roof crane lies across the river on the Helix terrace. First floor: the middle has
  fallen (walk it in 2049), the 2049 glass wall and the alarmed fire door are passed in 2196 (four switches). Second
  floor: storm cells, the Corran & Vey manifest (Helix moved "Artefact A", the ring, down the Eastway over the Varrow
  fill in 2031: the fill was built to carry the Chronolith), the north stair that only exists in 2049. Third floor: it
  collapses section by section (a run to the roof stair). On the roof the Witness stands on the terrace and is gone. On
  the boom the rest of the tower falls.
- **V · Test Hall C (2196 / 2049).** The cracked Chronolith, a seized Continuum stabilizer holding the hall in one
  year, Sera, Wren and Joss. Kai gives Sera the anchor's heart; she seats three couplings while Prefect Adair's troopers
  come through three breaches in three waves (non-lethal combat: stun, displace, restrain the ones who yield); troopers
  at the spire pull the field down. Between waves collar C slips "in your year": Kai reseats it at the 2049 console
  (run sheet: it slipped at 08:14:00.2, operator K. VAREN). Faction support from earlier choices joins wave three. The
  ring sings, and in it a voice says "Kai". The pattern is not in the ring: it is being carried by the Helix Long Array.
- **VI · The Long Array (2196 / 2049).** Halcyon Hill in a planetary storm. Gusts every 15 s (a ladder climb started
  before a gust is torn off the rungs). The south ladder to platform one; the 2196 ladder above is gone, so the 2049
  service lift ("Band recognised. Operator: K. Varen. Welcome back, Doctor Varen."); the 2049 radome is sealed, so the
  2196 north ladder to the crown. The carrier hums three notes (Mira's tune, E-B-A): tune the west, north and east
  horns. Residue Vision shows the Witness tuning them in iteration 1,046: "She called me up here too... Then I went back
  down and tried again." Mira's pattern appears:
  **"I remember the first world." / "Which one?" / "The one before you tried to save me."**
  She says the storms are the worlds Kai left open, and that the rest of her is "in a second nobody wrote. Between the
  ninth and the tenth": **the Unwritten Second**. The band receives the coordinates (19.10.2026, 08:14:09, then a second
  with no number); the storms stop for one second everywhere; a city nobody built appears in the clouds (M19).

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing crane over Lantern Hall, letterbox, chapter card ("ACT III · The Fracture"), then a card per place.
2. **Evacuate a settlement before its anchor fails**: anchor HUD (72 % falling, Joss's shunts +11 % each), the tally
   board (21 -> 34), four groups with four different problems (fetch, Residue search + choice, escort, persuasion
   choice), the storm front visibly approaching, the escape run.
3. **Cross a canyon that exists only in the past**: 2049 tear / 2026 gorge, hoarding bypass, generator, ramp with storm
   cells, crane, river (fall = checkpoint), hoist, ladder, the final 2049 step.
4. **Navigate a flooded highway in the present**: surge cycle and high ground, the lorry (two routes), live water and
   E-7, the bus, the pump, the 2026 culvert (a small change in the past that transforms the present), the drain.
5. **Traverse a collapsing future skyscraper**: 12-second 2049 holds, the four-switch floor, the 2049-only stair, the
   collapse run, the crown falling behind the boom.
6. **Defend a stabilizer while Sera repairs the Chronolith**: three waves, stabilizer and repair bars, the 2049 collar.
7. **Reach a planetary transmission tower**: gusts, ladders, the 2049 lift, the horns, Mira's message, the ending.
