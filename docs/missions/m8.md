# Mission 8 · The First Death

Act I · The World That Remembers. Purpose (bible): reveal that the present has changed. Bible target 45 minutes;
campaign plan about 55-60 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m08_first_death.js` (script, echoes, board, escape), `src/world/env_m8.js` (map, cast, music,
code-drawn screens), `tools/tests/m8.json` (playthrough, `?m=7`).

## Story
Kai comes back from the Sky House to Helix on a rainy Thursday night, 21.10.2049, and finds a memorial for himself
at the doors: "Dr. Kai Varen, 2018-2049. The first life lost to the Zero Second. He held the anchor so the city could
keep its second." In this version of the present he led the Chronolith programme, and the staff directory lists a
colleague he has never met, Dr. Yara Lind (containment physicist), missing since 08:14. Her badge trail and his own
voice memo ("If the field holds, nobody remembers there was anything to hold... Yara, don't follow me in") lead Kai
through the security office and maintenance to the containment antechamber. Through the observation window he finds
her, stopped mid-stride inside the containment field, three days inside one second. The field won't release without a
verified record of 08:04-08:14, and SEC-LOG 1 is corrupted. In Residue Vision Kai re-syncs the six cameras: the other
Kai running in, Yara ordering the antechamber sealed (Hale or Brandt, by flag), every bus routed to containment ("Good.
Nobody should watch this."), a long-coated figure at the door ("Ten minutes. It is always ten minutes."), "seal it
behind me", and 08:14 itself. He orders them on the board and opens Chamber One. Yara: "Don't save me." / "Why?" /
"Because I am not the one who died." The facility identifies its occupant as VAREN, K., status deceased, and seals the
chamber to collapse the field on the anomaly. Kai releases three clamps between sweeping field beams, then chooses
whether to take Yara's hand. In the antechamber the timeline audit lights up: "Current timeline: VERSION 17. No
surviving record of the previous sixteen." Niko's "You are the seventeenth" was a count of versions, not visits.

## Map (north = -z)
Street and rain-soaked forecourt with the HELIX monolith, planters, the gate kiosk and the memorial (portrait, candles,
flowers, three cards). The glass podium lobby (7.2 m atrium, light rings, reception, directory kiosk, founding plaque,
logo wall, turnstiles). The research corridor: security office (monitor wall, log server, reconstruction board),
records, Lab B (jammed blast door), Dr. Lind's office, Kai's office, maintenance (breaker panel). The loading yard and
the service passage (east). The containment antechamber (console, two observation windows, blast door, the audit
screen) and the round Chamber One (vessel, rings, three clamp alcoves at W/N/E). One temporal state (2049); Residue
Vision (F) shows the previous version: the old logo, plaque, staff list, "STORAGE 2B" on Kai's office door, the
drawing on the lift-shaft floor, the keypad presses.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Establishing**: crane from the street over the forecourt in the rain, letterbox, chapter card, Kai arrives out
   of the switch. Staged memorial reveal (portrait close-up, heartbeat, silence).
2. **Investigate an altered facility**: directory and plaque are required; the logo, Kai's office plate, the framed
   drawing, records, the kiosk log, the scientist and three memorial cards are optional. Kai's card at the turnstile:
   "HOLDER DECEASED". `trustedHale` true/unset: Hale (night shift) remembers Checkpoint C and lets him through.
   `trustedHale === false`: the guard refuses; the route is the loading yard, a service keypad whose code (0814) only
   shows in Residue Vision, the service passage and maintenance.
3. **Find a missing researcher**: Lind's desk (badge 4471: 08:02 office, 08:06 security, 08:09 maintenance, 08:12
   antechamber) and Kai's voice memo; the log server is dark; the breaker panel in maintenance (A/B/C/D: only A+B can
   come back; D keeps the field fed) restores the log and wakes two corridor drones (stealth, room checkpoints, Hale
   or Brandt on the radio). The antechamber window: a staged reveal of Yara frozen in the field.
4. **Recover a corrupted security log**: the console refuses without a record; the powered server lists six lost
   segments; each is re-synced at its source camera in Residue Vision (cameras pulse cyan) and plays as an echo scene
   seen from the camera, with Kai's and Yara's echoes and the figure.
5. **Reconstruct the final ten minutes before the Zero Second**: the reconstruction board lists the six segments
   shuffled; the order follows Yara's badge times and what is said in each clip; a wrong pick is explained, never
   punished. Kai narrates the restored ten minutes.
6. **Escape a temporal containment chamber**: the quote scene with staged close-ups; the system seals the chamber
   ("Identity: VAREN, K. Status: deceased"), two field beams sweep the floor, a 2:00 collapse timer runs, three clamps
   (hold E) sit in alcoves the beams can't reach; each clamp speeds the sweep and raises the music. Three beam hits or
   the timer: fail and restart at the chamber door with released clamps kept.
7. **Ending**: the choice (take her hand / do as she asks), the VERSION 17 audit on the antechamber screen, Kai on
   Niko's count, Hale (trusted) or Brandt on the radio (untrusted), fade, completion card.

Music: `m8-altered` (familiar Helix pad with one wrong note), `m8-hunt` (drones), `m8-echo` (log playback),
`m8-containment` (cello ostinato that climbs per clamp), `m8-version` (memo, quote, ending).

who kept the drawing; Hale or the night shift), the kiosk log, the scientist, personnel records (every record has a

scheduled the test, the other Kai wrote the containment plan and Rook claims it) and a choice (team first / park
the drones) that gives his override.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Start; inside the gates; every room once the drones are active (drone detection restores to the room you came from
and resets the patrol); after the power; the antechamber; the chamber door during the escape (clamps kept, timer and
hits reset). The breaker panel, keypad, intercom, console and board can be reopened; a wrong board pick or code is
explained and retried. Hints come as Kai barks after 25-110 s without progress and name the next place. Choices
change dialogue and flags only; neither route nor outcome blocks the campaign.
