# Mission 23 · Afterzero (Finale)

Finale. Purpose (bible): resolve the emotional conflict and leave a sequel hook. Bible target 90 minutes (the task brief
asks for about 117 with the optional content); estimate 85-110 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m23_afterzero.js` (script, endings, credits, post-credit clock), `src/world/env_m23.js` (the merged
landscape, three epilogue sets, the clock scene, cast, ten music themes, code-drawn signs/mural/clock faces),
`tools/tests/m23.json` (playthrough, `?m=22`). Reuses read-only helpers: env_m6 `Batch`, env_m18 `signTex` / `paperTex`,
env_m19 `shardMesh` / `pageMesh` / `veilMaterial` / `fragments`, props `streetKit` / `Walker`, kit `chronolith`.

## Story
The second started (M22): 08:14:10. Every place the Witness ever broke has drifted in around the torn floor of Test Hall
Zero, and Mira waits at the original Chronolith, the ring the keepers found and Helix found again. The Witness goes
ahead to the Confluence, where the places meet before a fracture wall with three seals: each place is "holding a
second" and gives up a thread. Kai carries four threads: the 08:14 passengers (the opening streets), the keepers'
memory (the settlement), Sera's tomorrow (the future city) and, at the end of the laboratory, the Witness's 1,046
attempts. Behind the wall, Helix's laboratory closes behind anyone who walks it: Kai and the Witness take turns holding
it open, and at the seam the Witness stays ("I was in every second of this. Let me be in one I chose."). At the ring
Kai tells Mira the bible line, decides what to preserve and what to release, and chooses the ending.

## Map (north = -z, one scene + three epilogue sets + the clock)
- **The hall** (z 48..74): the floating floor of Test Hall Zero, the cracked ring on its cradle, a bridge of setts,
  asphalt, ward tile and stone.
- **I · The opening streets** (z 8..48): M1's avenue in three local states (keys 1/2/3): `a26` building site and
  hoarding, `a49` the Zero Second (rain, Checkpoint C sealed, frozen commuters, the stalled 08:14 tram, Hale's and Jun's
  echoes, the station clock at 08:14), `a96` overgrown ruin (fallen overpass with one low gap, the fallen bronze
  statue, a sinkhole across z 12..20).
- **The Confluence** (x -16..16, z -14..8): four pavings meeting in seams, the clock with no hands, drifted props
  (tram shelter, well, barricade, gurney), the fracture wall (veil between broken arches) with three seals.
- **II · The settlement** (west, x -62..-16): houses (realism `settlement`), the ember bowl, three hearths with carved
  sun / ring / river stones, the mural wall (panel two scraped; Residue Vision shows its paint), the stream (no
  floor), the stepping stones, the shrine of standing stones with the tally of seventeen, the Helix card in the
  threshold stone.
- **III · The future city** (east, x 16..64): a 2196 street, a parking deck (y 6) reached by a ramp with the survivors'
  roof garden, the skybridge over a bottomless fissure in two local states (`f96`: 6 m gap x 37..43; `f49`: whole but
  shuttered at x 44), Lantern Hall's roof with the beacon mast, Sera and the camp.
- **The laboratory** (x -8..8, z -14..-54): D1 (held by the Witness), the lab hall with three lanes of equipment
  sliding backwards, the anchor console and plate, the seam (z -37..-46) of paving that falls upward, D3.
- **IV · The original Chronolith** (z -54..-92): a stone terrace in a ring wall where every era collides (keepers'
  houses west, a 2196 ruin east, 2049 and 2026 towers north); the ring upright on its cradle, Mira, the threads table,
  the three ways (white plinth A, red plinth B, the ring itself C).
- **Epilogues** (`buildEpilogue`): A Rain City on a clear morning, clocks running, a bench; B the same street cut by a
  seam of light, 2049 west and 2196 east, far flashes where the borders are fought over; C Calder Row at dawn (2049).
- **Post-credit**: a clock face in the dark.

Budgets (gfx=low, measured in the playthrough at 960x540 and 844x390): 34-150 scene draw calls and 100k-330k
triangles in play (the avenue in 2049 is the heaviest view, ~92 calls once settled; up to ~260 for a moment while a
cutscene's wider zone radius is still applied), well inside 250 calls / 700k triangles. Zones hide beyond a per-zone
radius (people beyond 60 m), 1 shadow caster, 3 nearest point lights + sun + hemisphere.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Opening** (story setup): black → crane over the merged landscape, letterbox, chapter card FINALE · Afterzero ·
   Choose what to preserve; band "08:14:10. Elapsed: one second"; the ring far north; the Witness ("Every place between
   here and there is one I broke"), reacting to `m22together`.
2. **Cross fragments of the opening streets** (I): chapter card; Checkpoint C blocks the street differently in each
   state (gate 2049, hoarding 2026, rubble 2196 with a crawl gap: Kai crouches by himself); past the rubble the 2196
   road is a sinkhole, so he must switch before stepping on (switch rules: never into something solid, never onto
   nothing). Thread: the 08:14 passengers in the stalled tram (2049). Optional: Hale's echo (`trustedHale` changes it),
   Jun's echo (bible bark "My phone says 8:14. The station board says tomorrow."; `metJun`), the fallen statue, the
   2026 site board, a shard (2026), a page.
3. **The Confluence**: chapter card, the Witness explains the seals and the folded laboratory; four optional topics
   with the Witness; the clock with no hands; a shard; touching the wall lists the missing threads.
4. **Cross fragments of the settlement** (II): chapter card; take an ember; light the hearths in the old order (sun,
   ring, river). The mural's middle panel is scraped; Residue Vision shows a broken ring. A wrong order puts the fires
   out (Niko's echo: "Not that one."; after two misses the hint points to Residue Vision). The stepping stones rise
   ("Water crossing stone."). At the shrine Tala and Niko: "You came before the first fire." / "That is what the
   others said." / "Sixteen came before you... The stars-count knew you were the seventeenth (as in M4). You are
   the first who asked us something." Thread: the keepers' memory. Optional: the keeper (bible bark "You wear no dust, but the old story knows your face."), the Helix card,
   a shard, a page.
5. **Cross fragments of the future city** (III): chapter card; up the parking deck; the skybridge: the 2196 gap cannot
   be jumped (6 m), in 2049 the bridge is whole but shuttered at x 44, so Kai crosses the gap in 2049 and the shutter
   in 2196. Sera (`seraTrust`, `m21ally`) asks him to hold the beacon's coil (hold interaction); the light comes back;
   thread: Sera's tomorrow; she lays a plank for the way back. Optional: the gardener (bible bark "The channel is
   running again. Nobody alive remembers who opened it."), the garden marker, a shard, a page.
6. **Cooperate briefly with the Witness to hold open a route** (the laboratory): the wall opens (staged). (a) The
   Witness holds D1 for 32 s while Kai crosses three lanes of reversed equipment and locks the anchor console (hold);
   touching equipment or running out of time fails back to D1 with the timer reset. (b) Kai stands on the anchor plate
   while the Witness crosses; leaving it for 2 s throws the Witness back to the door. (c) The seam: paving rises out of
   the dark, hangs, and drops; the Witness slows it; Kai hops across. (d) The Witness's sacrifice: an intent choice
   (come with me / you knew / what do I tell her), the fourth thread (his attempts), "Let me be in one I chose", he
   holds the seam and the last door opens.
7. **Reach Mira at the original Chronolith** (IV): establishing crane, chapter card; Mira asks what he came to do
   (ask / fix / unsure; `m20answer`/`m19miraAnswer` colour it); the bible quote **"I thought the answer was to find the
   right world." / "And now?" / "Now I think we kept asking the world to pay for our answer."**, one breath of
   silence. Optional: four topics with Mira (Calder Row, the hall, World 0907, "Would it be enough if I said that's a
   person?"), a shard.
8. **Choose what to preserve and what to release**: at the table, each carried thread is preserved or released (its
   light stays or fades); Mira comments on the balance. Then the three ways; each describes its cost and asks to
   confirm (Not yet returns to the choice).
9. **The ending** (player-selected): played at the ring (A the ring breaks and Mira fades; B the states split, the
   halos widen; C Kai steps into the ring and becomes the anchor), then its own epilogue scene with captions for each
   preserved / released thread and the bible quote: A "For the first time, tomorrow belonged to tomorrow." B "We had
   saved every possible tomorrow. None of them agreed on the future." C "I finally found a second that belongs to
   nobody." (C: Mira, 33, on Calder Row hears a whisper, "Live.").
10. **Credits** (a code-drawn roll over the final theme), then **post-credit**: a clock with no hands in the dark gains
    hands and starts to tick; the voice: **"Version one hundred and eighteen."** Completion card; the run returns to
    the title.

Music: `m23-confluence` (62 BPM, the main theme reduced), `m23-rain` (92 BPM muted pulse, drifting tick), `m23-past`
(82 BPM frame drum, flute, pluck), `m23-future` (70 BPM wide strings), `m23-witness` (54 BPM, the motif low on cello),
`m23-hold` (104 BPM, tightens with the timer), `m23-mira` (64 BPM felt piano, the E-B-A motif), `m23-final` (62 BPM
piano and cello, strings bloom per ending), `m23-war` (ending B colour), `m23-clock` (ticks only). Silence before the
quote, the whisper and after each epilogue quote.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Hall start; the avenue entry; the plaza; the settlement entry and the stream once the stones rise; the future city
entry and Lantern Hall's roof after the beacon; D1 (equipment hit or the Witness letting go restores here with the
timer reset); the plate (leaving it throws the Witness back, Kai keeps his place); the near ledge of the seam; after
D3; at the table; at the three ways. Falls into the stream, the sinkhole, the fissure or the seam return Kai to his
last safe spot (core rule) or the zone checkpoint. No soft-locks: wrong hearth orders reset only the fires, every
state can be switched back (stability regenerates; switching into a solid or onto nothing is refused with a toast),
Sera's plank keeps the way back from Lantern Hall open, "Not yet" returns to the choice.

## Testing

--outdir=<dir>` (and `844 390 --mobile`). The test exercises every optional item, a wrong hearth order, a switch the

the clock through to `m23done`. Development handles: `window.__m23` (G, S, X.W / witness / mira / holdT / plats / setZone).
