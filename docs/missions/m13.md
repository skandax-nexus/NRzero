# Mission 13 · The Nine-Minute War

Act II · The Nine-Minute War. Purpose (bible): deliver the first large-scale battle and a consequential choice. Bible
target 75 minutes (campaign plan 98); estimate about 95-110 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m13_nine_minute_war.js` (script), `src/world/env_m13.js` (map in three states, cast, music,
code-drawn boards and screens), `src/systems/factions.js` (shared faction support, owner M13), `tools/tests/m13.json`
(playthrough, `?m=12`). Uses `src/systems/combat.js` and `src/systems/echostep.js` (owner M10). Imports
`registerM10Cast` from `src/world/env_m10.js` so Prefect Ima Adair keeps her M10 look.

## Story
Friday 24.10.2049, 05:52. The Witness's trail from Chamber II leads Kai to the Sable Yards, a rail and tram district on
Rain City's east side that has been held in one second (08:14:00, 19.10) since the Zero Second: rain hanging in the
air, traffic and about three hundred residents stopped mid-step, a tram stopped at the interchange. Helix has cordoned it
(Hale or Brandt on the radio). In the held tram timetable Kai finds a note in his own, older handwriting: "Break it.
Whatever she tells you. — K." His band finds his signature in 2196 at the same spot, and in 2196 the Yards are a
battlefield: the Unwritten (Dov Lasker; "No history is final") behind a scrap barricade in the south, the Continuum
(Prefect Adair) behind pearl barriers under the broken viaduct in the north, and between them the Continuum's
stabilizer, a spire that pins the Yards in one second across their states. At full charge it would hold the district
for good, a fixed point. Every nine minutes a field "return" brings the sky down on both lines, and both answer.

Sera Voss is in the Unwritten camp walking civilians out of the middle (she returns Kai's displacer). Lasker offers
smoke if Kai pulls the stabilizer's three stakes. Kai crosses the yard, finds the field anchored in three states and
pulls them: the gold stake driven through the held tram driver's console (2049, guarded by Continuum Keepers who walk
in the held second; the held rain falls a centimetre), a "client fitting" in pile cage C-5 of the interchange's 2026
foundation works (the site manager Greta Oyelaran has a pour at 11:20; Kai finds the cage from the pile schedule, the
delivery note and Residue Vision, lifts it with the site crane and pulls the spike before the concrete ever buries it),
and the pylon on Sable Bridge (2196, a Continuum post with a shielded officer, flanked through the jammed 2049 bridge).

With the field down, both lines fall silent, and still a salvo comes out of the spire's ring. Kai reads the dud round
stamped with Lasker's own battery inside the Unwritten wall, the Continuum bolt scorched into the Continuum's own pearl,
and, in Residue Vision, the return ring swallowing four shells at 06:12 and throwing the same four back at 06:21. Both
factions are being shelled by their own previous attacks and answering them: "They're not fighting each other. They're
fighting themselves, nine minutes ago." He persuades Lasker (his own round) and Adair (her own fire record, exactly
09:00:00 between every OUT and IN) to hold fire for one loop. The last returned salvo lands and then nothing comes
back. On the plaza Sera: "This is not the future." / "What is it, then?" / "The future that survives long enough to
remember us."

At the stabilizer's heart (charge 94 %, 312 held in 2049) Kai chooses:
- **Destroy the stabilizer** (Lasker, and the Witness's note): overload three couplings and run before the spire comes
  down. The war ends, the Continuum loses its fixed point, the Unwritten owe Kai; the held residents in 2049 are still
  held. Sera: "I hope it was the right sum."
- **Redirect its energy to free the civilians** (Sera): turn three couplings to the ground; in 2049 the rain falls, the
  coffee cup finally lands, the tram pulls in, three hundred and twelve people finish their step. The spire stands empty
  in Continuum hands; Lasker feels betrayed.

From the broken viaduct the Witness watches: he nods (destroy) or turns his back (free), and changes state.

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing: crane over the held street, letterbox, chapter card, the coffee cup close-up, radio at the cordon.
   Walk in through the held: four optional "held moments", the Witness's note.
2. The band flares, forced switch to 2196: the war reveal (tracers out of the return ring, the Continuum address), the
   Unwritten camp, Lasker and Sera (staged two-shots), faction HUD and the first support offer, the displacer.
3. **Cross an active battlefield**: the barrage lands in the same holes in the same order (red telegraph rings, 2.2 s);
   a Continuum forward post (2 troopers) at the plaza edge; a Keeper patrols the held 2049 street (alternative route);
   Unwritten smoke (G / SUPPORT) hides Kai from Continuum eyes; Echo Step decoys; checkpoints at the gap, the tram
   wreck and the corner. The nine-minute field clock ends in a Return that rains on both lines.
4. **Disable three devices in different temporal states**: the dome cinematic and band reading; anchor 1 in 2049
   (stealth past two Keepers among the held, hold E 2.2 s, noise); 2026 opens; anchor 2 in 2026 (foreman scene,
   investigation, crane choice puzzle with retries, pull the spike); anchor 3 in 2196 on the bridge (frontal under the
   bridge barrage, or flank through 2049/2026 and switch in behind them; the officer's shield; Senn's hold-fire
   support). Each anchor is staged in the other states (rain drops fall, the dome shrinks, the address reports).
5. **Recognise that both factions are reacting to their own previous attacks**: the silent lines and the salvo from the
   ring (staged), three pieces of evidence (the dud round, the scorch with its Residue echo, the return ring in Residue
   Vision) plus optional tally and fire record, then a three-question deduction (wrong answers get Kai's reasoning and a
   retry, never a fail).
6. Persuasion: Lasker and Adair each need the right argument (their own round / their own record; asking works with
   standing >= 1; lying or threatening costs standing and they make you try again). One loop of silence: the last
   return lands, the clock stops, the fighters stand up. The quote with Sera (staged singles, silence).
7. **Choose to destroy the stabilizer or redirect its energy to free civilians**: the heart, both arguments (Sera,
   Lasker on the radio, the Witness's note if read), the choice, three couplings in the lit order; destroy = escape run
   (22 s, retry from the deck) and the collapse; free = the held second lets go in 2049. Consequence scenes in both
   states, the Witness on the viaduct, completion card with the faction standing.

Music: `m13-held` (one held second: glass harmonics, no pulse), `m13-camp` (the Unwritten and Sera), `m13-war`
(118 BPM, 16- and 14-step bars so the downbeat slips, brief gaps, rising "reversed" glass before impacts; layers by
threat and barrage), `m13-site` (2026 working morning), `m13-truth` (a nine-step figure against a 16-step bar, gains a
note per clue), `m13-silence` (the ceasefire), `m13-choice` (piano and cello), `m13-free` (strings bloom),
`m13-break` (low and heavy, then air). Music follows the era: 2049 always plays the held theme, 2026 the site theme.

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
The cordon, the barricade gap, the tram wreck and the corner on the crossing (shell hits and stuns put Kai back at the
last cover; the barrage restarts its cycle), the plaza edge (anchor 1), the site (anchor 2), the plaza east (anchor 3)
and the bridge approach, the bridge end, the evidence hunt, the talks, the spire stairs, the heart (the destroy escape
restarts from the deck). Wrong crane cages, wrong deductions and failed persuasion are retried, never failed. Era
switches into walls or over nothing are refused; 2026 explains why it is closed until anchor 1; shifting behind the
Continuum line is refused. Hints come as barks after 40-90 s without progress.
