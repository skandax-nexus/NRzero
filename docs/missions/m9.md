# Mission 9 · Nine Minutes to Zero

Act II · The Nine-Minute War. Purpose (bible): introduce knowledge-based looping. Bible target 60 minutes; campaign
plan about 75-80 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m09_nine_minutes_to_zero.js` (script, loop timeline, patrols, puzzles, ending),
`src/world/env_m9.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn textures), `src/systems/loops.js` (extended: wall-clock loops,
`skipTo`, `LoopPatrol`, `syncClocks`), `tools/tests/m9.json` (playthrough, `?m=8`).

## Story
Wednesday 22.10.2049, three days after the Zero Second and the morning after Helix (M8): the Meridian district of Rain City sits behind a Helix cordon. Inside it is always
Sunday 19.10.2049, 08:05:00 to 08:14:00: the nine minutes before the Zero Second, over and over. Kai crosses the
boundary at 08:09:40 (the radio from the cordon dies: Hale, or Helix security if `trustedHale === false`, and Yara if
`m8yara === 'saved'`). White-armoured Continuum troopers patrol a square full of people living the same morning. At
08:14:00 the district resets and takes Kai with it: he is back at the boundary at 08:05 with only what he knows. The
public-address speakers carry the voice: "You have nine minutes." / "To do what?" / "To become me."

The Continuum hold the district with an array: three public clocks (the station, the bank, the Market Hall's trigger)
kept in step by a transmitter mast in the substation. Ines Calder, a city grid engineer, is locked in the substation
cabin (its blast door runs off the mast) and remembers every loop. Her plan: knock all three clocks out of step in
one loop so the array loses its lock, pull the mast's three cores while the gate guard is away, free her, and reach
the ring on Meridian Tower, the only place above the reset, before 08:14. On the ring Kai finds the anchor: his own
Chronolith band, decades older, scratched "K.V.". At 08:14:00 the reset washes over the district below; Kai and Ines
stay at 08:14:01. A long-coated figure on the station clock does not reset either. The Continuum: "Two signatures
outside the loop. They remember. Retrieve them." (hands off to M10's capture).

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. **Establishing**: crane over the district from behind the tower, letterbox, chapter card, the clocks, the boundary;
   Kai steps through and the radio dies. Loop 1 starts at 08:09:40.
2. **Observe two loops and map enemy patrols**: climb the station stair, watch the square through the roof scope
   (staged scope shots of A, B and C). Loop 1 ends in the first reset (freeze, silence, rewind) and the PA quote scene.
   The patrol map needs Patrol A, Patrol B in an odd and an even loop (on the bank steps) and Patrol C leaving the gate
   at 08:11, so it takes at least two more loops of watching. Waiting at the café or the roof bench skips to times Kai
   already knows (knowledge-based waiting).
3. **Find three synchronized clocks**: Clock A's cabinet names the array and the three nodes ("C reset reference
   08:05:00"); Clock B's cabinet on the bank portico (only when Patrol B isn't standing on it); Clock C on the Market
   Hall gallery after the shutter opens. The engineer is found through the knocking at the cabin (north lane intercom).
4. **Sabotage the central transmitter**: in one loop, hold Clock A's relay, pull Clock B's fuse outside B's window, set
   the trigger to the 08:05:00 reference (wrong answers are explained, never punished); the array loses its lock and
   the shield drops; pull the three cores while Patrol C is away. The mast dies, the alarm music starts, two alarm
   patrols take the north lane.
5. **Rescue a trapped engineer and reach the tower before reset**: the cabin door opens, Ines follows Kai (she limps),
   past the alarm patrols to the tower lift (her code 0814), up to the ring. Any detection or 08:14 resets the run
   (clocks, cores, shield, Ines) but keeps every piece of knowledge.
6. **Ending**: Kai puts his band to the anchor ("K.V."), waits out the last minute with Ines, the reset wave washes over
   the district below, "It reset." / "I didn't.", the long coat on the station clock, the Continuum's order to
   retrieve them, fade, completion card.

  residue at the tower lift (Residue Vision: the long-coated man anchoring the first loop at 08:13:59). All five give
  the conclusion "The Ninth Minute".

- **Café Ostra**: Dev notices a little more each loop; the radio bulletin changes through the nine minutes.
- Five temporal shards (Lantern Alley, the station roof, the fountain, the Market Hall, the tower plaza).

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
Failure is the loop itself: being seen by a patrol (detection meter full) or 08:14 rewinds to 08:05 at the boundary,
soft fade, no flash; the loop rewinds the run state (clocks, shutter, back door, cores, shield, Ines, alarm) and keeps
every learned fact (the HUD ring marks learned times). The reset waits for a running conversation. Hints are Kai
barks after 40-160 s without progress and name the next place or time; a toast teaches waiting after the second reset.
No choice blocks the campaign. During letterboxed scenes the loop HUD keeps only its ring and label (knowledge lines and
the objective fade out; `loops.js` style).

## Playtime estimate
Arrival and loop 1 (stair, roof, first reset, the voice) 8 min · mapping the patrols over two loops with exploration
14 min · the three clocks and their cabinets 10 min · finding Ines and her plan 6 min · practising and failing parts
of the run (typically 2-3 loops) 15 min · the full run, rescue and tower 9 min · ending 4 min. Main path about 60-65
minutes; with the Ninth Minute evidence, Rhee, the mother, Dev and the shards about 75-80 minutes.
