# Mission 11 · The Inverted Hospital

Act II · The Nine-Minute War. Purpose (bible): a tense non-combat puzzle mission; introduces Inversion. Bible target
50 minutes (campaign plan 65); estimate about 60-65 minutes with the optional content below.

Files: `src/missions/m11_inverted_hospital.js` (script), `src/world/env_m11.js` (map, cast, music, code-drawn signs
and screens), `src/systems/inversion.js` (shared Inversion system), `tools/tests/m11.json` (playthrough, `?m=10`).

## Story
Friday 22.10.2049, night, rain. Halden General (a fictional Rain City public hospital) has had rain falling upward past
its East Wing since 08:14 on the 19th. Twenty-three years ago Kai's parents were told Mira died on its fourth floor.
At the cordon Dr. Priya Raman (temporal medicine) explains: some rooms in the East Wing run forward, some run back,
and a heart monitor nobody can find is loudest at the doors and quieter the closer you get. Hale lets Kai through
(or, if Kai distrusted Hale in M2, Brandt holds the door and Raman sends Kai through the ambulance bay). In the lobby
a coffee pours itself back into its cup; a fallen ceiling panel blocks the East Wing door, and Kai's band reads the
field: he learns to reverse one selected motion (Inversion). He crosses the reversed East Corridor, where everything
thrown at 08:14 keeps returning to its original position and landing again; follows the tone through dark Ward 4 to
the bay where it falls silent (4-07: an empty, made bed that weighs 54 kg, occupied since 08:14 on 19.10.2026); in
Medical Records compares the census, the bed sensor, the pharmacy log and a 2026 night book page he un-burns with
Inversion, and identifies the missing patient (and that Helix hid her). He dismantles the Helix reversing lock on
Restricted Records in the reverse of its fit order. Drawer 4-07: the records system says "Temporal discontinuity."
VAREN, MIRA · age 32 · record created 19.10.2026, twenty-three years before the present, when she was ten · date of
death: blank. The East Wing lets go and runs forward again; for a moment Kai sees Mira (a violet residue) sitting on
bed 4-07. "Nobody ever wrote down that she died."

## Beats (every bible bullet is playable)
1. Establishing: rain city at night, letterbox, chapter card, the upward rain past the East Wing (staged), Kai's
   setup lines. Cordon scene (Raman + Hale or Brandt; the Brandt route is a short stealth-lite approach through the
   ambulance bay, being seen near the gap gets Kai pushed back behind the line).
2. Lobby: the un-spilling coffee (staged), the tone and its meter; Inversion unlocked on the fallen panel (tutorial).
3. **Avoid hazards returning to original positions**: the East Corridor's six hazards each cycle land → return to
   their 08:14 position → hold → land again; a floor ring under each glows and ticks just before it moves; Inversion
   reverses the selected hazard (a hazard coming at Kai goes back the way it came). Touching a moving hazard fails to
   the last checkpoint (corridor start, middle). Staged camera on entry.
4. **Follow sound that becomes quieter as Kai approaches**: the 4-07 monitor tone, inverted acoustics (loud at the
   doors, silent at the source), a SOUND meter and a visual pulse for accessibility, barks when it swells (wrong way),
   the ward score thins out as Kai gets close, the reversed intercom handover ("Seven bay. Seven bay.").
5. **Compare medical records to identify a missing patient**: census, bed sensor, pharmacy log, the night book (un-burn
   it with Inversion) pinned on the incident board; two deductions (who is missing, who hid her); wrong answers get
   Kai's reasoning, never a fail.
6. **Dismantle a reversing lock in the correct order**: five parts (fit order core pin, cam plate, lower bolt, upper bolt,
   seal ring; remove in reverse). A wrong part makes every removed part fly back. Clues: the mirror-written plate
   "LAST IN · FIRST OUT", the Helix work order, and the technician's residue (Residue Vision) fitting it on a loop.
7. **Ending**: drawer 4-07, "Temporal discontinuity.", the file (age 32, record 23 years old, death date blank), the
   wing running forward again, Mira's residue on bed 4-07, Raman, completion card.

Music: `m11-rain`, `m11-lobby`, `m11-inverted` (phrases played back to front), `m11-ward` (thins out near the silent

## Optional content
Five temporal shards (chapel altar, the corridor alcove under the fallen panel — invert it and grab it before it comes
down again, Bell's bedside table, the burn bin after the page is restored, the vault). **The Man Who Says Goodbye
First** (Arturo Bell, bay 4-03, the Ward 4 orderly in 2026): his bay runs backward and he speaks his lines in reverse
order; invert his clock and his bay runs forward for 16 s: the girl in seven drew a black ring, said her brother would
come back looking younger than her, and a grey man with a scar visits every October. The chapel: Elena Varen's book of
intentions (19.10.2026) and, in Residue Vision, Elena and seven-year-old Kai in the pew. The Helix liaison office:
Rook's 09:02 memo ("classify as system failure… do not move the occupant of 4-07") and the 4-07 visitors' log ("K.
Varen, brother", every 19 October 2027-2048, older than his ID photo, same scar: Witness foreshadowing).

## System: `src/systems/inversion.js`
`Inversion(game, { cost, duration, range, key })`: readable zones (`zone({ min, max, dir, label, doors })`, threshold
chevrons that scroll the way time runs, a faint membrane, a HUD badge "◁◁ REVERSED · WARD 4"), tracks
(`track(obj, { from, to, arc, spin | pose, schedule | drift + latch | step, hazard, collide })` with u = 0 the original
position), selection ring + dashed path back to the origin, `invert(track)` (X / touch INVERT, stability cost,
`invertFor` per track), `sound({ pos, maxD })` inverted acoustics with a SOUND meter and pulse. Later missions (M21
"use inversion knowledge") can `new Inversion(game).unlock()` when `flags.inversion`.
