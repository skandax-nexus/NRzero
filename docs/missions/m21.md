# Mission 21 · The Last Battle of the Continuum

Act IV · The Unwritten Second. Purpose (bible): bring factions and combat systems together. Bible target 75 minutes
(the task brief asks for about 98 with optional content); estimate 80-100 minutes, see below.

Files: `src/missions/m21_last_battle_continuum.js` (script), `src/world/env_m21.js` (map, cast, ten music themes,
code-drawn screens and plates), `tools/tests/m21.json` (playthrough, `?m=20`). Reuses `src/systems/combat.js`,
`echostep.js`, `factions.js` and the helpers `kit`, `signTex`, `paperTex`, `shardMesh`, `stormWall` from env_m18.

## Story
Three days after Kai came back out of the Unwritten Second, the Continuum took the repaired Chronolith from Test Hall C
into the Bastion, their command grafted onto the shell of Helix Tower One. Prefect Ima Adair will fire it once at
nightfall, "the Last Lock": one timeline forever, every branch pruned (and with them what is left of Mira). Dov Lasker
has charges under the Bastion to destroy the whole anchor network. The Witness walked in through the front gate the
day before and has been "singing" to the ring. Sera gives Kai a displacer at the Unwritten forward post.

Kai gets over the wall by going through 2049 (Helix's plaza fence has one gap, at the loading dock, under a sweeping
camera), takes the door seal from the yard officer, fights the first Warden of the Fixed Point in the Register (the
Hall of Fixed Points, Helix's lobby in 2049) and two waves on the Proving Floor (the old Grand Atrium). Then the
Witness shows himself on the Cradle and Lasker blows the floor. During the collapse Kai locks seismic damper N2 in 2049
(the floor stops falling in 2196), balances the Halo sculpture's ballast in 2049 (the Cradle hangs on the same anchors
and stops listing), and sets transfer beam TB-7 by crane in 2026 (it is still there, rusted, across the chasm in 2196;
a green-ink note on the site board shows the Witness was there first). On the dais Adair makes her last stand with two
Wardens; Kai calls the ally his earlier choices earned. Then the Witness: a mirrored duel, the confession ("I was trying
to find a version of me that didn't have to." / "And did you?" / "No."), and he begins the final experiment: the ring
wakes, the earlier places (Central Station, the settlement, the observatory, a Rain City street) gather around the
Cradle, and he steps into 19.10.2026, 08:14. Kai follows ("Not without me"). M22 starts at the activation point.

## Beats (bible "Playable sequence")
0. **Intro** (2196, dusk): establishing shot over the ruined avenue to the Bastion, chapter card, Adair's address to the
   city, Sera and Lasker (radio) set up the three sides, Mira's voice through the band, the displacer.
1. **Infiltrate the command structure.** The Approach (two troopers patrol outside the wall in 2196) and the Bastion wall
   (no gaps in 2196). In 2049 the Helix plaza fence has one gap at the loading dock under a camera whose footprint sweeps
   the gap (a detection bar; caught = checkpoint). The muster yard: three patrols, supply-pod cover; Prior Halvard paces
   by the door with the seal. Take him down from behind (walk the forecourt in 2049, come back to 2196 behind him) or
   stun and restrain him; the seal opens the door.
2. **Fight elite enemies who rewind injuries and restore armor.** Wardens of the Fixed Point: two gold plates (a stun
   cracks one, shown on a label and on the suit), a suit that keeps its last three seconds (a stun with no plates left is
   rewound after 1.8 s and the plates come back; out of combat for 9 s it restores a plate). A displacement (hold) throws
   the three seconds away for 12 s ("REWIND BROKEN"), then stun and restrain. One Warden in the Register, one in Proving
   Floor wave 1, two in wave 2, two in the last stand (seven with Adair's guard counted as the HUD total). Era switching
   is locked during fights.
3. **Solve a multi-stage causal puzzle during an arena collapse.** Stage 1: the outer floor strips fall into the chasm one
   by one as the collapse meter rises (220 s); the survey plate on N2 (2196) says its damper failed open in 2049; in 2049
   four dampers (three locked, N2 open); lock N2 and the floor in 2196 holds. Stage 2: the Cradle gauge (2196) lists
   18° east; the Halo sculpture in 2049 hangs on the same anchors with ballast trays (west 10 kg, east 40 + 20 + 10 kg; the
   sign says "keep the trays equal"); the Halo tilts visibly in 2049 too; a tear meter fails the stage if it lists too
   long. Stage 3: the light-bridge fell; the empty seats are stamped TB-7 (2196); the 2049 skybridge plaque says TB-7 was
   set by hand on 21.10.2026, which unlocks 2026 here; the crane panel slews the beam (west / centre / east) and lowers
   it; only centre (the set-out paint on the slab) seats it; in 2196 the rusted beam spans the chasm. Falling debris
   (telegraphed rings) in 2196 throughout.
4. **Call on an ally earned through earlier choices.** Adair, two Wardens and three troopers hold the dais. The list of
   allies comes from flags (choice if more than one): `trustedHale` (Hale in 2049 cuts the old Helix grid: the Wardens'
   plates go and cannot come back), `m10released` (Teo and the freed prisoners stun the troopers), `m13choice` 'destroy'
   / `unwritten-sappers` (Maeve blows the anchor pylon: no rewinds), `m13choice` 'free' / `sable-civilians` (service
   codes: troopers' emitters jammed, the Wardens lose Kai), `m13senn` 'helped' (Senn orders the troopers to stand down),
   `m15choice` 'free' / `anja-diagnosis` (Anja's null frequency: no rewinds, one plate down each), `m15choice` 'stabilize'
   / `harrow-lantern` (the Lantern holds a second: everyone stunned 9 s, no rewinds), Sera's crew (if nothing else, or
   `seraTrust` ≥ 1). Called with the factions system (G / touch SUPPORT). If Hale was not trusted, Brandt speaks for Rook.
5. **Confront the Witness.** He comes down on the Cradle lift. A mirrored duel: he blocks stuns from the front (red
   shield), shoots like Kai, and after each hit rewinds three seconds (a red afterimage stays where he stood); flank him,
   displace him (drops the shield for 3 s) or leave an Echo Step for him to shoot. Three hits. Then the confrontation and
   the bible quote, a line that depends on `m20answer`, Adair's warning, the ring waking, ghosts of earlier places, the
   Witness stepping into the first second. Fade, results card.

Cinematic: establishing shot and chapter card, letterbox for every scene, staged shots (the forward post, the Bastion

## Checkpoints / failure recovery
The forward post (the camera or a fight in the Approach), the yard entry (in the era Kai came through), the Register
entry (the Warden fight), the Register exit, Proving Floor waves 1 and 2, collapse stage 1 (meter and floor strips
reset), stage 2 (tear meter reset), stage 3, the beam, the dais (the ally offer is re-armed if it was not used) and the
duel (one hit is forgiven). Combat checkpoints restore Kai's health and cells and put standing enemies back on their
posts; Wardens get their plates back. Falls into the chasm return Kai to his last safe spot. Wrong dampers, ballast moves
and crane positions are recoverable and only cost time. Era switching never puts Kai inside something solid or over
nothing.

## Optional content
Six temporal shards (beside the burnt tram 2196, under the guard booth 2049, behind the yard pods 2196, the Register
alcove 2196, the atrium planter 2049, the site's scaffold tower 2026). Conversations: Sera (trust-dependent), Maeve
Dunn and Lasker's charge map, the archivist Recorder Ilse Maren (let her go: Continuum +1 and her key to the alcove with
Mira's violet star clip; or restrain her). Evidence: the loop-aware trooper's diary, the Order of the Last Lock, the
corrected worlds (Sable Yards and Harrow Reach as Kai left them), the Register (VAREN, K. and "the elder... let him"),
Rook's 2049 transfer memo, the survey plate, the skybridge plaque, the green-ink note on the 2026 site board, the
violet star clip. Side quest **The Held**: three people held as fixed points in the Register; their pod labels name
anchor sockets L-3, L-5, L-1; the Helix lobby floor services panel in 2049 has six sockets; cut the right three and they
walk free in 2196 (Unwritten +1).
