# AFTERZERO production plan: 25–30 hour campaign

Target from the gameplay bible: 23 story missions (~25.5 h) plus 10 optional missions (~5 h),
across nine 3D regions with authored past/present/future states.

## New systems by act (from the bible's progression table)
- Act I: Local Loop Observation (M6), celestial alignment (M7), persistent consequences across eras (M5, M8).
- Act II: knowledge-based looping (M9), Echo Step (M10), Inversion (M11), faction support and the first large battle (M13).
- Act III: survival traversal, unstable temporal zones, memory/evidence triangulation, rewards from choices.
- Act IV: rule-breaking navigation, subjective memory puzzles, final causal synthesis, three endings.

## Delivery waves
1. Foundations (in progress): realistic humans, voices, mobile, physics, comfort UI, realistic maps for 1–4.
2. Act I completion: missions 5–8 with the Village, Clockmaker's House, Observatory and altered Helix maps.
3. Act II: missions 9–13 with the Loop City, Continuum block, Inverted Hospital and the war zone.
4. Act III: missions 14–18 in 2196.
5. Act IV + Finale: missions 19–23 and all three endings.
6. Optional missions and completionist content; full-length playtest and balancing to the 25–30 h target.

Durations are design targets; real playtime is measured during wave 6.
